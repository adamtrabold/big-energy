# Player review, round 11: T4 (three oscillators) and T2 fixed (four oscillators)

**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Reviewer:** independent critic, playing synths for decades (Model D, Prophet, Juno, Minibrute, Grandmother, OP-1, Make Noise and Mutable modules)
**Judged from:**
- the T4 flat top view and standing view;
- the T2 (fixed) flat top view.

I judged fresh.

**The bar:** the owner's 10, meaning "I'd play this happily on stage and in the studio, and I'd defend every control's place."

**Plainly:** T4 is good. I'd play it, and I'd defend every control's place to the owner, at a 9. Its base is the best base this project has had. Two things stand between it and a 10:
- the bend stick lowered to 9.0 mm;
- the lid's air sitting in two unrelated places.

T2 is a 7. It keeps the round-10 base problem (the mod switches far from MOD). But it has a fix that makes its base T4's base plus one mixer knob, so the four-oscillator build can converge on T4 too.

## T4: what works

1. **The base is right in every place:**
   - The MOD group (blend · pitch · filter) sits directly over BEND and MOD. BLEND is over BEND and PITCH is over MOD, so "set up the wobble" is one left-hand area.
   - KEYS (voices · velocity · touch) sits next to it, with about 19 mm between the groups.
   - MIXER 1 · 2 · 3 sit exactly over RESONANCE · CUTOFF · ENVELOPE.
   - The ECHO and REVERB levels sit exactly over VOLUME and PAN.
   - Both rows end at the same margin.

   I'd defend every knob and switch on it.
2. **The lid's spine:** AMP across the top centre, the speaker, then FILTER at the bottom (nearest reach), directly over CUTOFF. That's the matched A · D · S pair around the speaker.
3. **The effects sit on the right:** ECHO in two rows (type · time · free/sync; repeats · pan), with REVERB at the bottom right, all over the base's effect levels.
4. **Every split lines up across the hinge:** filter over cutoff, the effects over their levels.
5. **TUNING sits in the bank,** in oscillator 1's row (tune in the empty pitch slot, drift beside it), under a TUNING label. That's the owner's R2.

## T4: what still doesn't work

1. **The bend stick lowered to 9.0 mm.** You can see it in the standing view: the stick is a stub next to the MOD knob. Bend is the control you grab blind mid-phrase, so this is a real playing cost, not a detail. When the case closes, the stick lands under oscillators 2 and 3's OCTAVE column, because the bank starts about 13 mm from the lid's left edge.

   **Fix (as in P5OBD, round 5):** shift the oscillator bank right about 20 mm, so the stick lands in the oscillator-number margin. To make room, move **TUNE · DRIFT down to row 4, under the bank.** After the shift, DRIFT in row 1 would crowd AMP's ATTACK.
   - This also fills the empty lower-left quadrant (item 2).
   - Oscillator 1's pitch slot goes back to honestly empty, as on the Model D.
   - **The team should check two things:** that the stick clears with the bank shifted, and that the closing heights work for lid row 4's knobs over base row 1. The FILTER row already sits over the mixer, so I expect it to.

2. **The lid's air sits in two unrelated places:** the lower left, under oscillator 3 (about 80 × 35 mm), and the right at row 3, between ECHO and REVERB. One gap of air reads as intended; two in different places read as leftover. Fix 1 removes the lower-left gap, leaving one band of air: right, row 3, the separator between the two effects. The bottom row then reads TUNING · FILTER · REVERB, and the top row reads osc 1 · AMP · ECHO. I'd defend that.

## T2 fixed: what works and what doesn't

**What works:**
- the four-oscillator bank;
- the FILTER contour over CUTOFF;
- ECHO in two rows on the right, over the effect levels;
- MIXER 4 present.

**What doesn't work:**
1. **The base puts the MOD group about 100 mm from the MOD knob** (blend · pitch · filter in the middle of row 1), with the mixer at the left over the controllers. That's the round-10 problem, unchanged. The team says the T4 base doesn't fit with four oscillators: the mod and keys switches overlap MIXER 1.

   **Fix: don't start the mixer over TRACK.** Put **MIXER 1 · 2 · 3 over RESONANCE · CUTOFF · ENVELOPE, exactly as in T4,** then **MIXER 4 · EXTRA · SOURCE continuing right.** MIXER 4 sits over the gap between ENVELOPE and TAP, and SOURCE ends short of the ECHO level (about 20 mm by my estimate). That frees the TRACK column on row 1 for the left groups. The space from the left edge to there (about 90 mm) holds BLEND, five switches at 11.25 mm, and the 6 mm group gap. **The four-oscillator base then becomes T4's base with one knob appended:** the same muscle memory for either build. The team should check the SOURCE-to-echo-level clearance.
2. **TUNING is at the top centre, about 55 mm from the bank.** Put TUNE in oscillator 1's empty pitch slot, and DRIFT in the same header, as P5OBD solved it (or under the bank, if row 5 exists).
3. **The lid's bottom band and lower right are empty,** because REVERB floats below the FILTER row instead of on it. Put REVERB on the FILTER row (the bottom row: osc 4 · filter · reverb), and AMP to the top centre over the grille (the spine), with ECHO at the top right in rows 1–2. That's T4's lid with one more oscillator row.
4. **The stick at 9.0 mm.** Same fix as T4: shift the bank right about 20 mm. There's room: about 35 mm between TRACK 3 and the grille.
5. **The base's empty hinge band** (about 28 mm). Accepted before for the deeper face; it barely shows when standing.

---

## Where it stands

- **Three oscillators: T4 is good (9).** With the bank shift and TUNE · DRIFT under the bank (fixes 1–2), the stick returns to full height, and the lid's air becomes one deliberate band. Then it's my 10.
- **Four oscillators: T2 converges on T4,** with the mixer starting over RESONANCE, plus the spine, REVERB on the bottom row, TUNING in the bank, and the bank shift. I'd expect a 9 at once, and a 10 once it's checked.
- Either way, the owner gets one layout logic for both builds. That's worth more than either layout alone.

## Top fixes

1. **T4:** shift the oscillator bank right about 20 mm so the closed stick lands in the number margin, and restore the stick to full height.
2. **T4:** move TUNE · DRIFT to row 4 under the bank. That leaves one air band (right, row 3).
3. **T2:** start MIXER 1 over RESONANCE (as in T4), with MIXER 4 · EXTRA · SOURCE continuing right. Then the MOD and KEYS groups fit at the left over BEND · MOD.
4. **T2:** use the lid spine (AMP top centre, FILTER bottom), ECHO at the top right, REVERB on the bottom row, TUNING in the bank, and the same bank shift.

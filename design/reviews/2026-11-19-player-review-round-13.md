| Layout | Score | Based on |

T4 is the one I'd defend control by control. I tried to break it below and couldn't find anything that costs the player. T2 improved, but it still has a playing cost the team says it can't remove, and its right column has gone off the grid.

## What works and what doesn't (most important first)

### T4: argued against, and it holds up
1. **Echo is where the hand goes.** Time · repeats · free/sync sit on the lid's bottom row, the row nearest the player. That's what taxonomy §3/§10 asks for. In the standing render, the move from cutoff up to time is short and direct, and the echo level throw sits right below it on the base. Reverb (pure set-and-forget) takes the far top corner. Correct.
2. **Every lid knob is on one row grid.** Rows run at the same four heights across the whole lid: oscillators, amp and reverb on the top row, echo type · pan on oscillator 3's row, then tuning, filter contour and echo time · repeats on the bottom row. Nothing floats any more.
3. **Rule H, where it matters most, is exact:**
   - The filter contour sits directly over resonance · cutoff · envelope.
   - The amp contour sits directly over the filter contour, attack over attack. Two envelopes in the same columns is the most readable envelope arrangement I've seen on a compact synth.
4. **The vibrato gesture is in one hand.** Mod knob front-left, with blend · pitch · filter directly behind it under the "mod" sub-heading. You hold mod up and flip pitch on with the same hand, as the Model D teaches you to.
5. **Labels and headings are now one system.** Every word sits above its control. Main sections use one heading style. Tuning, mod and keys get the smaller ruled sub-heading, and each one is a sub-category in the taxonomy (2e, 1b, 1c), so the hierarchy is true as well as tidy. "Mixer" now carries a full heading, so 1 · 2 · 3 no longer read as part of the filter column.

**Objections I raised against T4, and why I let each go:**
- *The echo character sits one column left of the echo / reverb levels (rule H only roughly met).* Echo needs three columns (time, repeats, free/sync); reverb and the levels need two. No arrangement lines all of them up. The effects block sits over the effects levels as a whole, and that's how a player reads it. **Accept.**
- *Free/sync is two columns from time, with repeats in between.* It's a set-once switch. Time's printed scale (16 · 8 · 8. · 4 · 2 · 1) already shows the sync divisions, so you never need the switch while turning time. Time → repeats is the order every delay since the Space Echo uses. **Accept.**
- *Link sits beside volume, not pan.* Volume and pan are fixed under the owner's echo / reverb levels, and there's no room to the right of pan. Link is set-once and sits inside the "out" group. **Accept.**
- *The mixer sits under the filter, not under the oscillators.* Mixer level is the drive control, and in T4 it shares a hand position with cutoff (the Model D's own adjacency). This still satisfies the owner's rule R1 (mixer on the base). **Accept** (argued in round 12).
- *Track 3 sits alone in a fourth column; osc 1 has an empty pitch slot.* Both are on the grid. Track 3 sits on oscillator 3's own row, and osc 1 has no pitch knob on the Model D either. **Accept.** It's the one spot the owner might call "random" again (he used that word for the track control on p50), so be ready with that answer.
- *Empty cell between reverb and echo in the right column.* It separates two effects that you use differently (one ridden, one set), and it keeps the top row reading oscillators · amp · reverb. **Accept.**

### T2: what it fixed and what it can't
1. **The vibrato split remains (the main cost).** Blend · pitch · filter sit at the back-centre, about 110 mm from the mod knob at front-left. The team is right that four mixer channels plus extra and source leave no room for them at the left without moving the echo / reverb levels, which the owner has fixed. I checked the front row gaps too (release to track, envelope to tap, tap to link): none takes three switches. So it's a real constraint, and the cost stays. Blend also still sits in the filter column, between the contour and cutoff.
2. **The right column is off the grid and crowded.** Amp, reverb, echo and echo again: four rows stacked where the rest of the lid has three plus a bottom row. Those rows don't line up with the oscillator rows; the bottom row (echo time) sits about 5 mm below osc 4 and the filter contour. Reverb is also stepped one column right of amp and echo, so the column zigzags. The owner has rejected this kind of thing before.
3. **The amp contour is now over the effects**, far from the filter contour. The envelope parallel is lost, and rule H suggests a link between amp and the effects levels that doesn't exist.
4. **Tuning is centred over the speaker**, about 60 mm from the oscillators. That only loosely meets R2 ("closer to the oscillators").
5. **Fixed since last round:** the blank row above osc 4 is gone; echo time · repeats are on the near row; labels and headings match T4's system; the mixer sits under the oscillators (rule H met, and the one thing T2 does better than T4).
6. **The bend stick is lowered to 9.0 mm** to close at T2's height. A shorter throw is less precise for bends by hand.

## Owner objections to expect
- **T4:** "Why is track 3 off on its own?" (answer above). Possibly "there's a gap over echo" (answer above). Nothing I'd expect him to push on after that.
- **T2:** "The mod switches are nowhere near the mod knob." · "The right side is crammed and doesn't line up." · "Tuning is decorative."

## Suggestions
1. **Take T4 to the owner as is.** Show VCO 4 as the option it is (taxonomy Q12 default). I'd only want T2 if VCO 4 were a must-have, and then the price is the split vibrato gesture and a crowded right column.
2. **If T2 stays in the set**, put its right column back on the lid's row grid (amp, reverb, echo, echo on the same row heights as the oscillators, or drop to three rows), and line reverb up with amp and echo. That won't fix the mod split, but it removes the visual objection.
3. **Nothing else for T4.** Have the "track 3 / empty cell" answers from above ready when you present it.

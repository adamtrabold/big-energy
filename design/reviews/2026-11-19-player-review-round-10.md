# Player review, round 10: T1, T2, T3 (laid out from the approved taxonomy)

**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Reviewer:** independent critic, playing synths for decades (Model D, Prophet, Juno, Minibrute, Grandmother, OP-1, Make Noise and Mutable modules)
**Judged from:** the flat top views of T1, T2 and T3, and the T1 standing view. T2 and T3 share T1's base apart from MIXER 4, so the T1 standing view stands for the base in all three. I judged fresh, against the taxonomy the owner approved.

**The bar:** the owner's 10, meaning "I'd play this happily on stage and in the studio, and I'd defend every control's place."

**Short version:** the taxonomy is visible in all three, and the best idea is new. In **T1, every group that's split across the hinge lines up across it:**
- the oscillators sit over their mixer;
- the filter contour sits over CUTOFF;
- the effects' character sits over their levels.

That's the rule from round 9 applied everywhere. None of the three is a 10 yet, though. T1's lid has empty corners, and TUNING sits away from the bank. All three bases send the mod switches far from the MOD knob. **I'd push T1** (or T2, if the owner keeps VCO 4), with three fixes. Both converge on the same lid.

## What works (keep it, in all three)

1. **The splits line up across the hinge** (T1 and T2 especially; seen in the T1 standing view):
   - the oscillator bank on the lid's left over the MIXER on the base's left;
   - the FILTER contour under the grille, directly over RESONANCE · CUTOFF · ENVELOPE (FILTER DECAY in line with CUTOFF);
   - ECHO and REVERB on the lid's right over the base's ECHO and REVERB levels.

   You can read the instrument top to bottom by category. Nothing earlier did this as completely.
2. **Branch 1a is together on base row 2:** BEND · MOD · GLIDE · RELEASE. The left hand's playing controls sit in one place, with RELEASE beside GLIDE, as the taxonomy now says.
3. **KEYS (voices · velocity · touch) is its own group,** and the gap between it and MOD (about 18 mm) tells your fingers which group they're on. That fixes the round 8 complaint.
4. **The right column lines up:** AMP decay/sustain, echo repeats/pan and REVERB sit in the same two columns, over the base's effect levels and VOLUME/PAN.
5. **T3's TUNING under the oscillator bank** is the cleanest answer yet to the owner's "master tuning … closer to the oscillators."

---

## What doesn't work, most important first

### 1. The base separates the MOD knob from where mod goes (all three)
MOD (the depth) sits at the far left of row 2, by BEND. BLEND and the PITCH and FILTER destination switches sit in the middle of row 1, about 100 mm away. The taxonomy splits them into 1a and 1b, which is fine in the tree. But its own conflict table (§4) argues that "set up the wobble" should be read in one place. Bringing vibrato in by flipping PITCH with MOD already up is a left-hand move you make without looking. Here, your left hand has to cross to the middle of the panel.

**Fix:** put the MOD group (BLEND · PITCH · FILTER) at the left of row 1, directly over BEND · MOD · GLIDE · RELEASE, with KEYS beside it, separated by its gap. Then put **MIXER 1–3 · EXTRA · SOURCE back over RESONANCE · CUTOFF · ENVELOPE**, so the mixer stands over the filter it feeds, as in P5RS. The effect levels stay over VOLUME and PAN.

**What you give up:** "oscillators over mixer" becomes "oscillators over the controllers." I'd make that trade. Mixer-over-filter is real signal flow, and the mixer is often ridden with CUTOFF (pushing levels into the ladder for grit). The oscillators-over-mixer alignment is a pleasant column coincidence: mixer 1 sits under the octave column, not under oscillator 1.

### 2. T1's lid: TUNING is in the wrong place, and the lower corners are empty
TUNING sits at the top centre, about 55 mm from the bank. The lid has two holes:
- under oscillator 3 (about 75 × 30 mm);
- to the right of the FILTER row and below REVERB.

**Fix (it borrows from T3, and from P5OBD's spine):**
- **Left:** oscillators 1–3, with **TUNE · DRIFT in row 4 under the bank** (as in T3). That fills the lower-left and satisfies R2.
- **Centre:** **AMP across the top row, over the grille**, mirroring FILTER below it. The two envelopes form a matched A · D · S pair around the speaker. FILTER stays in the bottom row (nearest reach) over CUTOFF.
- **Right:** ECHO in rows 1–2 (type · time · free/sync; repeats · pan), and **REVERB in row 4**. The bottom row then reads tuning · filter · reverb, and the top row reads osc 1 · amp · echo.
- **Row 3 on the right is the one band of air,** separating ECHO from REVERB. That's a deliberate gap, the same as the version I defended in round 3.

Every split still lines up: FILTER over CUTOFF, the effects over their levels.

### 3. T2: the deeper face leaves bands of air
With KEYS gone from the lid, T2's four-oscillator lid has:
- an empty band about 25 mm deep along its bottom edge;
- a hole at the lower right;
- an empty hinge band on the base (about 28 mm), as P5OBD had.

**Fix:** the same spine as fix 2. AMP moves to the top centre, ECHO to the top right in rows 1–2, and REVERB to row 4 to complete the bottom row (osc 4 · filter · reverb). TUNING goes back into the bank, in oscillator 1's empty pitch slot, with DRIFT under the same header, as P5OBD solved it.

Before accepting the deeper face, check whether it's still needed for the stick's closing clearance. If not, take the depth back out.

### 4. T3: both envelopes in the top row
The idea, both contours side by side as one row, reads neatly. But it puts FILTER DECAY, the envelope knob you ride, in the farthest row, and it leaves the space under the grille empty. Keep T3's TUNING position; drop its envelope row.

### 5. The back-row gaps are uneven (all three)
On base row 1, the gaps are about 28 mm, 18 mm and 29 mm. Once fix 1 restores the column lock (mixer over filter, levels over out), make the rule "row 1 lines up with row 2," and let the gaps follow. The 18 mm KEYS–MOD gap should stay smaller than the gaps between major groups. That way it reads as "related groups" rather than "random."

---

## Which to push to 10

**T1 for three oscillators, or T2 if the owner keeps VCO 4.** With fixes 1–2 (or 1 and 3 for T2), both arrive at the same structure:

- **Lid:** oscillators and tuning on the left; AMP · speaker · FILTER as the centre spine; echo and reverb on the right.
- **Base:** MOD and KEYS on the left over the controllers; the mixer over the filter; the levels over out.

Every split lines up, every group has a reason, and play controls stay a hand's width from the keys. I'd defend that to the owner as a 10. T3 contributes its TUNING position and should stop there.

## Top fixes

1. **Base:** move the MOD group (blend · pitch · filter) to the left of row 1, over BEND · MOD; KEYS next to it; MIXER 1–3 · EXTRA · SOURCE back over the filter row.
2. **T1 lid:**
   - TUNE · DRIFT under the oscillator bank (row 4);
   - AMP to the top centre over the grille;
   - ECHO to the top right in rows 1–2;
   - REVERB to row 4.
3. **T2 lid:** the same spine, with TUNING in oscillator 1's pitch slot. Recheck whether the deeper face is still needed.
4. **Base row 1:** lock its columns to row 2, and keep the KEYS–MOD gap smaller than the gaps between major groups.

# Project site critique with the nudge skill (2026-11-13)

Independent critic, run with the nudge skill (Shift Nudge's 16 principles). Reports to the owner; decides nothing. Judged screenshots of the live site taken before the fix pass: To decide (desktop and phone), Features (desktop and phone), Physical, Tests (phone), the Aftertouch doc, the CHAMBER group page and History → decisions. Desktop at 1440 px (1x), phone at 390 CSS px (2x).

**5 / 10**. The site is calm and neutral, but precision is missing and two phone pages are broken. Not defensible as is.

## What was off
- **Phone nav hides half the site.** Tabs stop at "Physical", and nothing shows that Electronics, Parts, Tests and History can be scrolled to. (14. Universal)
- **Tests table breaks on the phone.** Text spills into the label column, and URLs wrap mid-word. The one page he acts on is the hardest to read. (16. Responsibility)
- **No two page types share a left edge.** Header at 48 px; content at 318, 218 or 48 px; the back link and copy link touch the window edges; "7 decisions waiting on you" floats at about 560 px. (2. Grids, 7. Precision)
- **The header shouts and repeats itself.** Spaced all-caps status line, title, centred count, tabs. The count appears twice, and about 200 px of every phone page goes before any content. (12. Restraint, 8. Order)
- **The grey falls on the wrong things.** His own quotes on History are secondary grey, and dropped concepts look lighter than 3:1 (estimate). (3. Color, 15. Accessibility)

## Questions
1. What does a repo path tell him on Physical, or `rb.py` / Kalam notes on the CHAMBER page? (6. Clarity)
2. A doc that isn't written yet shows ten "To be written." headings. Would one line say more? (4. Communication)
3. Should "waiting on you" get the one Braun signal colour, and nothing else? (9. Affordance)

## Working well
Every page opens with one plain sentence saying what it's for (6. Clarity). The decided / proposed / open chips read in greyscale (15. Accessibility). The neutral grey answers "not cream". The quiet sans and hairline rules fit the brief.

## Structure notes (for the content team)
- Item 6 on To decide points to "Docs → MIDI", but there is no Docs tab.
- Item 7, "Site navigation", is a question about the site, not about the synth.
- Repo paths and pipeline notes appear in page content.

## What the visual pass changed (same day)
- One left edge on every page, including the back bar.
- Header in two lines. The status line is now sentence case on one line. "Waiting on you" sits on the right and carries the one green signal dot.
- On the phone, the tabs wrap so all eight sections show.
- Phone tables are fixed: each label sits beside its full value.
- Tap targets are at least 24 px, small labels went from 10.5 to 11.5 px, his quotes are in full contrast, and dropped items are easier to read.
- Left to the content team: the duplicate count, repo paths, stub docs, and items 6 and 7 above.

# Player review, round 8: P5RS

**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Reviewer:** independent critic, playing synths for decades (Model D, Prophet, Juno, Minibrute, Grandmother, OP-1, Make Noise and Mutable modules)
**Judged from:** the P5RS flat top view and standing view, judged fresh.

**The bar:** the owner's 10, meaning "I'd play this happily on stage and in the studio, and I'd defend every control's place."

**Plainly:** this is now a good instrument. I'd play it, and I'd defend it to the owner as it stands, at a 9. It's ahead of P5R as the three-oscillator build. Two things keep it from a 10:
- the six base switches need a real gap between the KEYS group and the MOD group (and a hand test);
- the owner needs to accept that TUNING has left the oscillator block. He once asked for it closer to the oscillators.

## What works now

1. **The top-centre void is gone.** TUNE and DRIFT form a centred TUNING pair above the grille. The top row now reads OSCILLATORS · TUNING · FILTER, and the pair sits like a crown over the round window, which suits the cathedral idea. Keeping the grille in line over CUTOFF was the right call over my suggestion to raise it. That line ties the lid to the base, and it's worth more than the space I wanted.
2. **TUNING no longer needs a joining rule.** Tune and drift sit side by side under one header, the plainest possible answer. Oscillator 1's empty pitch slot is honest (the Model D's oscillator 1 has no frequency knob). TRACK 3 is in oscillator 3's row.
3. **The base grid is back:**
   - MIXER 1 · 2 · 3 sit exactly over RESONANCE · CUTOFF · ENVELOPE, so the mixer stands over the filter it feeds.
   - The ECHO and REVERB levels sit exactly over VOLUME and PAN.
   - Both rows end at the same right margin.

   That's P5R's 9 base, now with the KEYS switches added.
4. **The effect levels are back a hand's width from the keys.** Echo throws are a play gesture again.
5. **The right column is one clean stack:** FILTER, AMP and REVERB on the same three columns. The Model D's filter-over-loudness order is kept, and REVERB is part of the column rather than floating.
6. **The ECHO strip** (type · time · free/sync · repeats · pan) runs under the oscillators and the grille. It's one whole group, nearest the hand on the lid.
7. **It closes with 5.0 mm clearance** between the base switches and the lid's echo knobs. That's enough.

## What still doesn't work, most important first

1. **The six base switches have no gap between groups.** RELEASE · VOICES · VELOCITY · TOUCH | PITCH · FILTER all sit at one 11.25 mm pitch. Only the printed rules tell KEYS from MOD. Your eyes can read the rules; your fingers can't. VELOCITY and TOUCH are set once per song, but PITCH and FILTER (where the mod knob goes) get flipped mid-song, often without looking. Six identical bats in a row, with about 2 mm between nuts, is where you flip TOUCH when you meant PITCH. The owner asked for tactile section-finding early on ("use another method of separation if you think it's important to feel the sections").

   **Fix:**
   - Open a gap of at least one extra half-pitch (about 6 mm) between TOUCH and PITCH, by moving the MOD group and BLEND about 6 mm right. There's room: BLEND to MIXER 1 is about 29 mm centre to centre today.
   - Do the hand test the team proposed, with the real toggles at 11.25 mm. If flipping one switch without catching its neighbour is unreliable, go to about 12.5 mm within each group and take the space from the BLEND–MIXER gap.
2. **TUNING has left the oscillator block.** TUNE now sits about 49 mm (edge to edge) from oscillator 1's WAVE, in its own group. As a player I'm fine with it: a master tune at the top centre is easy to find, and you set it once per gig. But on 2026-11-13 the owner said master tuning "should sit closer to the oscillators". This position is a step back from that. It's his call, not a layout fault, so ask him. If he wants it back in the bank, the honest place is oscillator 1's empty pitch slot (TUNE) with DRIFT under the same header, as in round 7, and the top centre goes back to air.
3. **The base back-row gaps don't match (15.1 against 28.6 mm).** They follow from locking row 1's columns to row 2: the mixer over the filter, and the levels over OUT. I'd defend alignment over equal gaps, and fix 1 changes them anyway. After fix 1, check that the gaps read as intended, and tell the owner the rule is "row 1 lines up with row 2."
4. **Minor: VELOCITY and TOUCH labels run to three lines,** taller than their neighbours' two. It's legible, so leave it, unless the owner flags uneven label heights.
5. **Minor: the bottom strip has a wide gap (about 56 mm) between echo PAN and reverb TYPE,** because REVERB lines up with the right column instead of sitting next to ECHO. I'd defend the column, since it keeps FILTER, AMP and REVERB as one stack. It just means the strip reads as "echo, left; reverb, right column," not one centred band.

## Where it stands

- **Three oscillators:** P5RS is the answer, now at 9 and ahead of P5R. With fix 1 done and hand-tested, and the owner happy with TUNING at the top centre, it's my 10.
- **Four oscillators:** P5OBD remains the 10.

## Suggestions

1. **Open about 6 mm between the KEYS and MOD switch groups,** then hand-test the real toggles at 11.25 mm (go to about 12.5 mm within groups if needed).
2. **Ask the owner:** TUNING centred above the grille (as now), or back in oscillator 1's pitch slot?
3. **After fix 1,** recheck the back-row gaps and state the rule that row 1 lines up with row 2.

# Player review, round 3: P5R and P5OB (amp row, grille, filter row)

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Reviewer:** independent critic, playing synths for decades (Model D, Prophet, Juno, Minibrute, Grandmother, OP-1, Make Noise and Mutable modules)
**Judged from:**
- the P5R flat top view (re-rendered this round) and the P5R standing view;
- the P5OB study flat top view.

I judged fresh, hands first.

**The bar:** the owner's 10, meaning "I'd play this happily on stage and in the studio, and I'd defend every control's place."

**Short version:**
- **P5R is now an 8 that's hard to push higher.** What holds it back is built into it: the oscillators run in one long row.
- **P5OB is a 7 with a clearer route to 9 or 10.** Its structure is the better instrument. What's left is a layout problem (one big empty band), and the fix for that band also answers the team's VCO 4 question.

## Scores

| Layout | Lid | Base | Overall | Based on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P5R** | 8 | 8 | **8** | flat top view and standing view |
| **P5OB study** | 7 | 8 | **7** | flat top view |

The base is the same in both, so it scores the same.

---

## What works (keep it)

### Both
1. **The MIDI dots are back, and consistent:** one dot right after the label (octave •, tune •, pitch •, pan •, mod •, glide •, cutoff •), on knobs only. A DAW-first player can see what the DAW can move.
2. **OCTAVE has position labels** (lo · 32 · 16 · 8 · 4 · 2), in the same one-line style as TIME and the TYPE lists. The whole panel now uses one vocabulary for stepped knobs.
3. **The base's OUT group is right:** VOLUME, PAN and LINK on one row under one OUT header.
4. **TAP is fixed.** It has a dark cap with the tempo ring, it no longer looks like a key, it sits beside the ECHO and REVERB levels, and its label is one word.
5. **MOD has a sub-header over PITCH and FILTER,** so PITCH no longer reads as "bend on/off."
6. **The base has a rhythm now.** On both rows the groups are split by equal gaps of about 37–39 mm. Last round it looked random; now it looks intended.

### P5R
7. **FILTER, AMP, ECHO and REVERB headers are all left-aligned over their groups.** The headers no longer sit on the DECAY labels. ECHO now clears the grille.
8. **The TUNING sub-header** separates TUNE and DRIFT from oscillator 1's own knobs.

### P5OB
9. **The filter is one column across the hinge.** With the lid standing, the FILTER envelope row (attack · decay · sustain) sits right above the base's filter group, and FILTER DECAY is exactly in line with CUTOFF. Your eye and hand go straight from cutoff up to filter decay, the two knobs you ride together. ECHO and REVERB on the lid's right also sit above the base's effect levels and TAP. **The lid's groups stand over the base groups they belong with.** P5R has nothing like this; its filter envelope is on the far left, a long way from CUTOFF. To me this is the strongest argument for P5OB.
10. **The two envelopes are a matched pair:** AMP above the grille, FILTER below it, both A · D · S. The filter row is the nearest reach on the lid, and the amp row is the farthest. That's the owner's layout, and it's also where a player wants each one.
11. **The oscillator bank:** three rows at an even pitch, numbers in the margin, all the PITCH knobs in one column for detuning by eye, and TRACK 3 in oscillator 3's row.

---

## What still doesn't work, most important first

### P5OB
1. **The right half of rows 2–3 is a void.** After REPEATS and PAN, the band from the grille to the right edge is empty down to REVERB, about 110 mm across at row 3, and more by the team's measure. There's a smaller hole between TRACK 3 and the grille too. A player doesn't need the controls packed tight, but the owner has rejected unresolved gaps twice ("massive horizontal gap", "the speaker doesn't feel resolved"). This one will go the same way.
2. **The fix for the void is also the VCO 4 answer.** The team says VCO 4 only fits if KEYS leaves the bottom row. Do that, and send KEYS into the void:
   - **Left:** oscillators 1–4 in four rows, the bank the owner was "fine" with, and what he asked to fit. Without VCO 4, row 4 on the left is the KEYS group, as now.
   - **Right, rows 1–2:** ECHO, as now.
   - **Right, row 3:** REVERB (type · decay), moved up under ECHO so the two effects form one block.
   - **Right, row 4:** KEYS (voices · velocity · touch).

   Then every quadrant is full. The bottom row is osc 4 · filter · keys, and row 3 is osc 3 + track · grille · reverb. The effects block stays over the base's effect levels and TAP. KEYS ends up at the lid's lower right, the corner nearest the base's OUT and TAP. That's fine for three switches you set before a song.
3. **Text collisions:**
   - The OCTAVE position line for oscillator 1 runs into the OSCILLATORS header.
   - The KEYS header sits about 3 mm under oscillator 3's OCTAVE knob.
   - The FILTER header sits 4.6 mm under the grille.

   With KEYS moved (fix 2), the first two go away. The third needs the grille nudged up or made slightly smaller. Get eng's view on open area before shrinking it.
4. **The bend stick lowered to 9.0 mm is a real cost to a player.** When the case closes, the oscillator bank lands right over the stick. That didn't happen in P5R, where the stick folds into empty lid space. A 9 mm stick can still play if the cap is concave and grippy and the throw stays the same as the taller stick. But bend is the one control you grab without looking, mid-phrase.

   **Ask:**
   - First try to clear the stick by layout: a blank margin on the lid where the stick lands. The oscillator numbers' margin column is already near it.
   - Only if that fails, accept 9.0 mm, and only after a printed mock-up thumb test.
   - If 9.0 mm feels toylike in the test, that's a point for P5R.
5. **The TUNING sub-header floats** between OSCILLATORS and AMP, at a different baseline from both. Sit it on the same baseline as the other group headers, or put it under OSCILLATORS as a sub-label.

### P5R
6. **The oscillators still run in a single 290 mm row.** The PITCH knobs of oscillators 2 and 3 are about 46 mm apart, and the owner said "the oscillators are really far apart." P5R can't fix this without becoming P5O. It's playable, but it's the objection I'd lose arguing in front of him.
7. **The oscillator numbers "2" and "3" sit in the gaps between groups.** "2" sits about as close to DRIFT as to oscillator 2's OCTAVE, so it reads as either. Put each number tight to its own OCTAVE knob, or under the group as a sub-header ("2"), never centred in the gap.
8. **The filter envelope sits far from CUTOFF** (lid far left against base centre), and the amp envelope sits far from VOLUME. Nothing on the lid lines up with its partner on the base.

### Base (both)
9. **The two rows don't end at the same place.** Row 1 ends at TAP (about 18 mm from the right edge). Row 2 ends at LINK, leaving about 40 mm empty at the right. Pull row 2's groups right to the same right margin, or spread them so the equal gaps reach both ends.
10. **The columns still don't line up between rows.** The mixer knobs sit 2–4 mm off the filter knobs below them. That's small, but at this bar, close-but-not-quite looks like a mistake. Line up MIXER 1–3 over RESONANCE · CUTOFF · ENVELOPE, or offset them on purpose by half a column.
11. **The MOD sub-header covers PITCH and FILTER but not BLEND.** BLEND is the mod source mix, and it belongs to MOD too. Extend the MOD sub-header across BLEND, so the controllers read as RELEASE | MOD (pitch · filter · blend) over BEND · MOD · GLIDE.

---

## Would I defend P5OB with those fixes? Yes.

With fixes 2–5 (four oscillators on the left, effects stacked on the right with KEYS under them, collisions cleared, and the stick question settled by a thumb test), P5OB is the one I'd argue *for*. Every group would have a reason for its place:
- The oscillators are the Moog bank.
- Each envelope is where your hand wants it.
- The filter runs as one line from CUTOFF up to its envelope.
- The effects sit above their levels and TAP.
- Play controls stay on the base, and settings stay on the lid.

I'd expect a 9. It reaches 10 when the base's ends and columns line up (fixes 9–10), and the stick is either kept at full height or proven playable at 9.0 mm.

P5R at 8 is a good fallback. Its stick doesn't need lowering, and that's a real point in its favour. But no amount of polish changes its oscillator row or its separated filter.

---

## Suggestions (in order of payoff)

1. **Render P5OB with four oscillators on the left, REVERB moved up to row 3 under ECHO, and KEYS at the bottom right (row 4).** Also show the three-oscillator version, with KEYS bottom left. That fills the void and answers VCO 4.
2. **Try to clear the bend stick by layout first** (a blank spot on the lid where the stick lands). Only then accept 9.0 mm, with a printed thumb test, same throw, concave cap.
3. **Clear the text collisions:** the OCTAVE line against the OSCILLATORS header, the KEYS header (solved by 1), and the FILTER header against the grille.
4. **Line up the lid and base on purpose:** put FILTER DECAY exactly over CUTOFF (it nearly is), and the ECHO and REVERB groups over the base's ECHO, REVERB and TAP. Make the alignment deliberate and exact, so it becomes the design rule the owner can see.
5. **Base: end both rows at the same right margin, line up the mixer over the filter row, and extend the MOD sub-header across BLEND.**
6. **TUNING sub-header:** put it on the shared header baseline.
7. **P5R only, if it stays in the running:** tie oscillator numbers 2 and 3 to their own OCTAVE knobs, not the gaps.

**Where I stand:** P5OB is the right instrument and isn't finished yet. Show me suggestion 1 and I'll judge it fresh.

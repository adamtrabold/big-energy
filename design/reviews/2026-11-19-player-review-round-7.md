# Player review, round 7: P5RS (from the owner's markup)

**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Reviewer:** independent critic, playing synths for decades (Model D, Prophet, Juno, Minibrute, Grandmother, OP-1, Make Noise and Mutable modules)
**Judged from:** the owner's markup (2026-11-19 13:53), and the P5RS flat top view and standing view, judged fresh.

**The bar:** the owner's 10, meaning "I'd play this happily on stage and in the studio, and I'd defend every control's place."

**Short version:** the owner's instincts are right. Stacking the oscillators fixes P5R's biggest problem, and a centred effects strip is a good idea. As built, it isn't a 10 yet:
- the lid has a large empty patch at the top centre;
- the base lost the grid that made P5R's base a 9;
- the effect levels went up to the lid, which the owner's markup didn't ask for.

All three are fixable. With the fixes below, this becomes the three-oscillator answer, and better than P5R.

## What works (keep it)

1. **The stacked oscillator bank (the owner's blue box).** Three rows, all the PITCH knobs in one column, TUNE in oscillator 1's empty pitch slot, DRIFT joined to it by the printed TUNING rule, and TRACK 3 in oscillator 3's row. This fixes the "oscillators really far apart" complaint at its root. It's the single biggest gain over P5R.
2. **FILTER envelope above AMP on the right (the red arrow).** That's the Model D's own order: filter contour above loudness contour. It's a convention players already know. FILTER DECAY is now the farther reach of the two, but it's only about 25 mm farther. I'd defend it.
3. **Echo and reverb as one strip, centred on the panel (the owner's "center?").** The strip runs from under OCTAVE to under SUSTAIN and is centred on the lid within a few mm. TYPE starts each group, FREE · SYNC sits between TIME and REPEATS, and the strip reads as one band: the effects.
4. **KEYS on the base (the pink arrow), under a printed "keys" rule, beside a "mod" rule.** RELEASE inside KEYS is defensible ("what happens when you lift a key"). The base's left side now reads as everything about how the keyboard plays, next to everything about where mod goes.
5. **Labels and markings** hold everything earlier rounds settled: one tick rule, the octave position lines, one MIDI dot style with a legend, one word per label.

## What doesn't work yet, most important first

1. **The lid has a void at the top centre.** Between TUNE and FILTER, above the grille, sits about 85 × 35 mm of nothing. That's the same "massive gap" the owner has rejected twice. There's a smaller hole on the right at row 3, between AMP and REVERB. It comes from honest arithmetic: KEYS left the lid, so there are fewer controls than space.

   **Fix: raise the grille into the top centre** so its top lines up with the tops of row 1's knobs (about 20 mm up). That fills the void, and a high round window suits a cathedral. The air that's left then sits in one place: a band between the voice section (oscillators, speaker, envelopes) and the effects strip along the bottom. That reads as a deliberate separation, not leftover space, and it explains the row 3 gap on the right as well. Check that the grille's sound path and the board under it allow the move.

2. **The effect levels moved up to the lid.** The owner's markup boxed echo and reverb with the controls they already had; it didn't move the levels. As a player I still want ECHO and REVERB level a hand's width from the keys: an echo throw at the end of a phrase is a play gesture. There's a fair counter-argument: an echo with time, repeats and level side by side is how most echo pedals work, and each effect stays whole. That's why I'm not marking it down hard.

   **But the levels' move broke the base,** which is the bigger cost (item 3). **Fix:** put ECHO and REVERB levels back on base row 1 at the right, over VOLUME and PAN, as in P5R. If base row 1 can't fit them (see item 3), keep them on the lid and accept the trade.

3. **The base lost its grid.** With the effect levels gone, the team slid the mixer right to fill row 1. Now MIXER 1 sits over ENVELOPE, and 2 and 3 sit over nothing in particular. The mixer no longer stands over the filter it feeds, which was the clearest signal-flow cue on the base. Row 1 also ends about 22 mm short of row 2 on the right. And the gaps run unevenly: about 23 mm between KEYS and MOD, about 38 mm between BLEND and MIXER.

   **Fix:**
   - Put MIXER 1 · 2 · 3 exactly back over RESONANCE · CUTOFF · ENVELOPE, with EXTRA and SOURCE after them.
   - Put the effect levels back at the right end over VOLUME and PAN (item 2).
   - Pack the seven left-hand controls (RELEASE, VOICES, VELOCITY, TOUCH | PITCH, FILTER, BLEND) on **one switch pitch** in the space left of the mixer. That's the owner's own note: "a tighter proportional grid if all the key controls are in one area."

   **By my count it's tight but it fits:** six switches at about 12 mm, one group gap of at least 6 mm, then BLEND, all within about 93 mm. If it doesn't fit, the fallback is the current mixer position, but then make row 1's ends and gaps regular on purpose.

4. **The KEYS sub-labels are crowded.** "velocity amp · both · filter" and "touch mod · off · filter" nearly touch at the current spacing. Whatever switch pitch you choose (item 3), keep at least 2 mm between neighbouring sub-labels, or put the position words under the switches, as P5OBD's keys do.

5. **Check that it closes.** Base row 1's switches now sit under the lid's echo row when closed: VELOCITY and TOUCH land near echo TYPE and TIME. My estimate is about 3 mm of clearance. The team should confirm the toggle height against the knob tops.

6. **Accept this:** the filter envelope is far from CUTOFF (lid upper right against base centre). That's the cost of the Model D filter-over-amp order. Only P5OBD's centre spine avoids it, and P5OBD needs four oscillators to fill its lid. I'd defend the trade for a three-oscillator build.

## Where it stands

- **Four oscillators:** P5OBD stays the answer (my 10).
- **Three oscillators:** P5RS, fixed, beats P5R.
  - With fixes 1–3 (grille raised, levels back on the base, base grid restored), I'd defend it at 9.
  - With 4–5 (sub-label spacing, closing check), it's my 10 for the three-oscillator build.
  - As it stands: 7.

## Suggestions (in order)

1. **Raise the grille** about 20 mm, into the top centre, and treat the remaining air as the band above the effects strip.
2. **Move the effect levels back to base row 1,** over VOLUME and PAN. Keep them on the lid only if row 1 can't take them.
3. **Rebuild base row 1:**
   - MIXER 1–3 back over the filter row;
   - KEYS and MOD switches on one pitch;
   - both rows ending at the same right margin.
4. **KEYS sub-labels:** at least 2 mm apart, or put the position words under the switches.
5. **Confirm closing clearance** between base row 1's switches and the lid's echo knobs.

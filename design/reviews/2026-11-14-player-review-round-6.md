# Player review, round 6: P5OBD

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Reviewer:** independent critic, playing synths for decades (Model D, Prophet, Juno, Minibrute, Grandmother, OP-1, Make Noise and Mutable modules)
**Judged from:** the P5OBD flat top view and standing view, judged fresh.

**The bar:** the owner's 10, meaning "I'd play this happily on stage and in the studio, and I'd defend every control's place."

| Layout | Lid | Base | Overall |

| **P5OBD** | 10 | 9 | **10** |

**Plainly: yes, this is a 10.** I'd play it on stage and in the studio. I'd defend every control's place to the owner, and I'm done arguing about layout.

The base keeps a 9 only for its empty band along the hinge. That's purely visual, it's hidden behind the lid's foot when standing, and the team gave a fair reason for it (152 mm halves otherwise). It doesn't cost the instrument a point.

---

## What I checked, and why it now holds

1. **DRIFT is no longer oscillator 2's knob.** The printed TUNING rule runs from the TUNING sub-header across and down to DRIFT, so one label visibly covers TUNE and DRIFT. It echoes the MOD rule on the base, so it's the panel's own language, not a one-off. It also doubles as a quiet boundary between the oscillator bank and the AMP row. Trim caps would have been my first choice, but the rule does the job, and it keeps the stick at full height. That's the right trade.
2. **KEYS has room to breathe.** Its words now sit under the switches, leaving 11.35 mm under REVERB's names. The right column reads ECHO, REVERB, KEYS as three clear groups.
3. **The outer margins match** (20 / 20.35 mm). The uneven gaps beside the grille now read as intended: the oscillators run up to the speaker, and the effects have room.
4. **Everything from the earlier rounds still holds:**
   - **Oscillators:** the four-oscillator Moog bank, with the PITCH knobs in one column, TUNE in oscillator 1's empty pitch slot, and TRACK 3 in oscillator 3's row.
   - **Envelopes:** AMP across the top (the owner's direction), FILTER below the grille as the nearest reach, and FILTER DECAY in line with CUTOFF across the hinge.
   - **Effects:** stacked on the right, above their levels, TAP and OUT.
   - **Base mixer and filter:** MIXER 1–4 sits exactly over the filter row.
   - **Base controllers:** the MOD destinations sit with MOD.
   - **Out:** VOLUME, PAN and LINK form one OUT group.
   - **Markings:** one tick rule, stepped knobs with one-line position labels, and one MIDI dot style with a legend.
   - **Bend:** the stick closes at full height.
   - **Lid and base:** settings on the lid, play controls on the base, with no exceptions.

## Optional polish (not a condition of the 10)

- **Switch labels sit on different sides.** TRACK 3, RELEASE, PITCH, FILTER and SOURCE are labelled above their switches. FREE · SYNC, the three KEYS switches, the base's TRACK and LINK are labelled below. Each choice makes sense where it is, and none of them confuses a player. But the owner has noticed inconsistent markings before, so if he raises it, pick one side (below suits the keys and the base row-2 switches) and apply it everywhere.
- **Settle "voices 1 · 2 · 4" in the feature doc** for the four-oscillator build.

## The owner's remaining call

This layout assumes **four analog oscillators and 145 mm halves** (+13 mm deep, the same closed thickness, about +55 g). If he keeps the fourth oscillator, P5OBD is the one. If he drops it, P5R (an 8) is the fallback.

# Player review, round 4: P5R and P5OBD (four oscillators, 145 mm halves)

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Reviewer:** independent critic, playing synths for decades (Model D, Prophet, Juno, Minibrute, Grandmother, OP-1, Make Noise and Mutable modules)
**Judged from:**
- the P5R flat top view (re-rendered) and the P5R standing view;
- the P5OBD study flat top view.

I judged fresh, hands first.

**The bar:** the owner's 10, meaning "I'd play this happily on stage and in the studio, and I'd defend every control's place."

**Short version:**
- **P5R's base is now a 9, and I'd defend it as it is.**
- P5R's lid stays an 8, for the same reason as last round: the oscillators run in one long row, and the filter envelope is far from CUTOFF.
- **P5OBD has the right lid structure, but it has a functional bug: oscillator 4 has no mixer level.** It also has a lowered stick, which I think one move can undo. Fix those and it's the one I'd argue for.
- **The choice between them is really the owner's VCO 4 decision.** Four analog oscillators means P5OBD. Three means P5R. The three-oscillator P5OB doesn't work, and I agree with the team on that.

## What works (keep it)

### P5R
1. **The base is finished as a layout:**
   - MIXER 1 · 2 · 3 sit exactly over RESONANCE · CUTOFF · ENVELOPE.
   - The ECHO and REVERB levels sit exactly over VOLUME and PAN.
   - Both rows end at the same right margin.
   - The groups are split by steady gaps (about 42 mm on row 1, about 30 mm on row 2).
   - The MOD rule now covers PITCH, FILTER and BLEND, so the controllers read as RELEASE | MOD over BEND · MOD · GLIDE.
   - TRACK has moved inside the FILTER group.

   Every control has a reason for its place, and the rows look planned. **This is a 9.**
2. **The oscillator numbers are now sub-headers** ("1", "2", "3" over each group). They no longer float in the gaps.
3. **A "• midi" legend** in the lid's corner explains the dot once. That's the right way to teach the convention.
4. **The bend stick folds into empty lid space at full height.** That's a real player point in P5R's favour.

### P5OBD
5. **The lid structure is right:**
   - the Moog bank of four oscillators on the left, all the PITCH knobs in one column;
   - the AMP row above the grille and the FILTER row below it, a matched A · D · S pair;
   - FILTER DECAY in line with CUTOFF across the hinge;
   - ECHO, then REVERB, then KEYS stacked on the right, above the base's effect levels, TAP and OUT.

   Every group sits above its partner on the base, or where the hand wants it. There are no text clashes.
6. **"voices 1 · 2 · 4"** finally makes sense here. With four analog oscillators, 2 voices × 2 oscillators is a musical mode. P5R says 1 · 3 · 4, so the feature doc and the panel must agree for whichever layout is chosen. Note this for the owner.
7. **The cost is honest and small.** It's 13 mm deeper per half, the closed thickness is the same, and it's about 55 g heavier. For a fourth analog oscillator, a player takes that trade.

---

## What still doesn't work, most important first

### P5OBD
1. **Oscillator 4 has no mixer level.** The base MIXER still reads 1 · 2 · 3 · extra. With four analog oscillators each one needs its own level, as on a Matriarch and as P5O had in round 1. Without one, oscillator 4 is either always full or always tied to another channel, and neither is acceptable. **The fix fits the grid:** MIXER **1 · 2 · 3 · 4** go exactly over **TRACK · RESONANCE · CUTOFF · ENVELOPE** (already four columns at the same pitch), with EXTRA and SOURCE moving one column right into the existing gap before the effect levels. Re-check that ECHO and REVERB still sit over VOLUME and PAN.
2. **The stick is lowered to 9.0 mm, and I think one move can undo it.** When the case closes, BEND lands under oscillator 3's OCTAVE knob, because the bank starts about 13 mm from the lid's left edge while the stick sits about 34 mm in.

   **Ask:** shift the whole oscillator bank right by about 20 mm, so the stick lands in the **oscillator number margin** (the "1 2 3 4" column), which has no knob.
   - There's room: about 50 mm sits between the PITCH column and the grille. After the shift, TRACK 3 still clears the grille by more than 10 mm.
   - The same shift fixes the panel's balance (item 3).
   - **The team should check:** stick footprint against the nearest knob when closed, and the closing heights.
   - If it works, the stick goes back to full height and P5OBD loses its last physical compromise against P5R.
3. **The lid is left-heavy, and the gaps don't match.** The oscillator bank starts about 13 mm from the left edge. The right block (echo, reverb, keys) ends about 40 mm from the right edge. The air beside the grille is about 50 mm on the left and about 30 mm on the right. That's the team's "uneven bottom-row gaps" (41.4 against 22.9). The 20 mm bank shift in item 2 makes the left margin about 33 mm against 40 mm on the right, and the grille's side gaps about 30 mm each. Then the seven "by-design gaps" become two matched bands of air either side of a central spine: **oscillators | air | envelopes and speaker | air | effects.** That reads as intended, and I'd defend it to the owner. Gaps of different widths, as now, look like leftover space.
4. **The base has an empty band about 25 mm deep along the hinge.** The extra 13 mm went to the top of the base, above CONTROLLERS, MIXER and EFFECTS. Spread it out instead: move both knob rows up about 6–8 mm, which adds the same to the gap between the knob row and the sharps. Players with big fingers will thank you; that clearance is where you clip ENVELOPE playing a C#. **Re-check where the stick lands** after moving it (item 2).
5. **Header baselines don't match.** AMP, OSCILLATORS, ECHO and TUNING sit on four slightly different baselines (TUNING is about 2–3 mm lower than the rest). Put all top-row headers on one baseline, with TUNING as a sub-header, the way MOD works on the base.

### P5R
6. **The oscillators still run in a single row.** PITCH 2 and PITCH 3 are about 46 mm apart, and that's the owner's "really far apart."
7. **The filter envelope is far from CUTOFF.** The filter envelope sits at the lid's far left, while CUTOFF is in the centre of the base. Nothing on the lid lines up with its partner below. Both of these are built into P5R. Polish won't fix them, and they're why its lid stays an 8.
8. **TAP's place is fine but unlabelled by group.** It sits between FILTER and OUT on row 2 with no header. The ring that blinks the tempo will teach it in a minute, so this is minor. If a header fits, put ECHO's tempo with the EFFECTS group, or leave it as is.

## Where I stand, and what the owner is really choosing

- **If the owner wants the fourth analog oscillator (he asked: "see if we can fit a fourth oscillator"),** P5OBD is the layout. With fixes 1–3 (mixer 4, the bank shift bringing the stick back to full height, and the matched air bands), I'd defend it at 9. With 4–5 as well, it's my 10. I'd play it on stage, and I'd defend every control's place to the owner.
- **If he stays with three oscillators,** P5R is the answer. Its base is a 9 now, and it closes with the stick at full height. Its lid is an honest 8 that I'd play happily, but I'd lose an argument about the oscillator row.
- **The three-oscillator P5OB is out.** The team is right that it can't fill its right side without putting levels or OUT on the lid, which breaks the one rule that makes this instrument easy to play.

## Suggestions (in order of payoff)

1. **P5OBD:** add MIXER 4. Put MIXER 1–4 over TRACK · RESONANCE · CUTOFF · ENVELOPE, and move EXTRA and SOURCE one column right.
2. **P5OBD:** shift the oscillator bank right about 20 mm so the closed stick lands in the number margin. If it clears, restore the stick to full height.
3. **P5OBD:** with the shift, match the margins (about 33/40 mm) and the grille's side gaps (about 30 mm each), and call the result "two bands of air around the spine."
4. **P5OBD:** move the base's two knob rows up 6–8 mm into the empty hinge band, for more clearance above the sharps. Re-check the stick after.
5. **P5OBD:** put all the lid's group headers on one baseline, with TUNING as a sub-header.
6. **Both:** settle "voices 1 · 2 · 4" (four oscillators) against "1 · 3 · 4" (three) in the feature doc to match the layout chosen.
7. **Owner question, not a layout fix:** four analog oscillators (P5OBD, 145 mm halves) or three (P5R, 132 mm). The layout follows from that answer.

Show me P5OBD with 1–5 and I'll judge it fresh. I expect that's the round I stop arguing.

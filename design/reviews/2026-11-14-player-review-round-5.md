# Player review, round 5: P5OBD (with the round-4 fixes) and P5R

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Reviewer:** independent critic, playing synths for decades (Model D, Prophet, Juno, Minibrute, Grandmother, OP-1, Make Noise and Mutable modules)
**Judged from:**
- the P5OBD study flat top view and standing view;
- the P5R flat top view and standing view.

I judged fresh, hands first.

**The bar:** the owner's 10, meaning "I'd play this happily on stage and in the studio, and I'd defend every control's place."

**Plainly:** P5OBD is now good. I'd play it on stage and in the studio, and I'd defend it to the owner as it stands, at a 9. One label problem keeps it from a 10: DRIFT now reads as oscillator 2's own knob. That's a small, specific fix (below). With that and a 4 mm nudge to KEYS, I'd call it a 10, and I'd stop arguing.

P5R stays a solid 8. It's the right answer only if the owner drops the fourth oscillator.

## What works

### P5OBD
1. **MIXER 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 sit exactly over TRACK · RESONANCE · CUTOFF · ENVELOPE.** Oscillator 4 has its own level, and the base grid is intact:
   - EXTRA and SOURCE sit to the right of the mixer.
   - TAP sits under them, with a TEMPO heading.
   - The ECHO and REVERB levels sit over VOLUME and PAN.
   - Both rows end at the same margin.
2. **The stick closes at full height.** The bank shift worked. Bend is the one control you grab blind mid-phrase, so this was the last physical compromise, and it's gone.
3. **Every group sits where a player would put it:**
   - the four-oscillator Moog bank, with the PITCH knobs in one column;
   - AMP across the top and FILTER below the grille, a matched A · D · S pair, with FILTER DECAY in line with CUTOFF across the hinge;
   - ECHO, then REVERB, then KEYS down the right, above the base's effect levels, TAP and OUT;
   - play controls on the base and settings on the lid, with no exceptions.

   I can defend every control's place, except DRIFT's (item 1 below).
4. **"voices 1 · 2 · 4" fits a four-oscillator instrument** (2 voices × 2 oscillators is a real musical mode). Make sure the feature doc says the same.
5. **The standing view reads well.** The oscillator bank fills the left of the lid, the speaker and the two envelopes form the spine, and the effects sit in a tidy column. On the base, CUTOFF sits under the filter row and the stick stands proud at the left hand.

### P5R
6. **The base is unchanged and still a 9.** The TEMPO heading over TAP is fine: a one-word header with a one-word label, just like OUT over VOLUME.

## What still doesn't work, most important first

### P5OBD
1. **DRIFT now reads as oscillator 2's knob.** It sits in row 2's fourth slot, right after "2 · octave · wave · pitch", while the TUNING sub-header sits only over TUNE in row 1. A first-time player will assume it drifts oscillator 2 alone. It's a global depth (every oscillator wanders). The cause is honest geometry: after the shift, the bank's fourth column only has room on rows 2 and 3 (rows 1 and 4 would hit the AMP and FILTER rows), and TRACK 3 must take row 3. So DRIFT has to sit on row 2, and the fix is in the labelling.

   **Fix (pick one, in my order of preference):**
   - **a.** Make TUNE and DRIFT visibly a different kind of knob from the oscillator knobs: smaller trim-style caps, or a cream skirt, on just those two. Global trims that look like trims are a convention players already know.
   - **b.** Draw the TUNING sub-header as a thin rule like the base's MOD rule, running from above TUNE across to above DRIFT, so one label visibly covers both.
   - Either way, don't add a second "tuning" word.

   With a, the lid is a 10 to me.
2. **KEYS sits 4.07 mm under REVERB's type names.** It doesn't clash, but at this bar it looks squeezed. There's about 14 mm of empty panel under the KEYS switches. Drop the KEYS header and switches by about 4 mm, to give at least 8 mm under the reverb names.
3. **The grille's side gaps are about 9 mm on the left and 28 mm on the right.** As a player I don't mind. The bank runs up to the speaker because its fourth column is a half-column of small things (DRIFT, TRACK 3), and the effects get room. I'd defend that to the owner as "the oscillators run to the speaker; the effects breathe." Two conditions: the **outer** margins must match (bank about 28 mm from the left edge, the right block about 34 mm from the right edge, so move the right block about 3 mm right), and DRIFT and TRACK 3 must not crowd the grille's outer bars. Keep at least 7–8 mm, which you have. If the owner still calls it uneven, the only real fix is a smaller grille, and I wouldn't trade speaker size for this.
4. **The base keeps an empty band about 25 mm deep along the hinge.** I accept the team's reason (raising the rows means 152 mm halves). In the standing view the band sits behind the lid's foot and barely shows. It costs nothing in playing; the gap between the knob row and the sharps is the same as P5R's. No action needed.

### P5R
5. **Unchanged from round 4:** the oscillators run in one long row (PITCH 2 and PITCH 3 are about 46 mm apart), and the filter envelope is at the lid's far left, away from CUTOFF. These are built into P5R, and they're why it stays an 8.

---

## What the owner is choosing

- **Four analog oscillators:** P5OBD, with 145 mm halves (+13 mm deep, same closed thickness, about +55 g). It's the better instrument. After fix 1 (DRIFT caps or a shared rule) and fix 2 (KEYS down 4 mm), I'd defend it to the owner as a 10.
- **Three oscillators:** P5R at 132 mm. It's a good, honest 8 that I'd play happily, but I'd lose the argument about the oscillator row.

## Suggestions

1. **DRIFT:** give TUNE and DRIFT smaller trim caps (preferred), or a single TUNING rule covering both.
2. **KEYS:** drop the header and switches about 4 mm, for at least 8 mm under REVERB's names.
3. **Right block:** move it about 3 mm right so the outer margins match the bank's.
4. **Feature doc:** make "voices 1 · 2 · 4" official for the four-oscillator build.

That's the end of my objections. Do 1 and 2, and P5OBD is a 10 from me.

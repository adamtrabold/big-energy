# CATHEDRAL panel vocabulary (director, 2026-11-13, after owner rule 19:57)

Owner rule (verbatim): "Labels should only be one word and follow best practices with obvious tweaks for understanding if there's a better word than the jargon"

One word per label. Headings carry the context, so a word repeats only under different headings (type, mix, pan, decay, track, mod). Standard words are kept where they're plain; swapped only where the jargon is clearly worse. Supersedes the earlier small label fixes 3 and 4. Fixes 1, 2 and 5 stand (out column on its line; the two lights labelled; touch legend ≥ 3 mm from the echo type names).

## Shared words
- Two "pan"s (echo, out), tied by the "link" switch and matching dots.
- Decays: two envelope knobs and the reverb knob; no switch says decay.
- "type", "mix" under echo and reverb; "track", "mod" on lid and base, always under different headings.

Not words: TIME's ms/beat scales and the 1/3 · 2/3 fractions (per spec).

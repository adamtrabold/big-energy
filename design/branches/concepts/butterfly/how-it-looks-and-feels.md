## Systems' changes: I accept them
- **The walnut is a 6 mm top laid in a rebate over the printed apron, with the v1.1 jacks below it** (systems §3).
  - It's better furniture than my drilled rail: a tabletop over an apron is the honest version.
  - The jacks sheltered under a live edge, "like a drawer under a tabletop", is a nicer detail than holes in wood.
- **The joint at y 165**, not my y 160: the type names would have been cut.
- **End match, not bookmatch**, and the butterfly genuinely holding an end butt: accepted. The precedent is unverified, and I say so.

## Principles
1. **Precision runs from front to back.** The key edge is exact; the face is exact and printed; the joint is exact and shown; the rear is the tree's own edge. One sentence, every line.
2. **The joint is the only ornament, and it works.** One straight joint, one butterfly across the leaves' end butt on the axis. Nothing else is added.
3. **Two materials, honestly next to each other.**
   - Matte **bone** PETG (#e2dacb drawn; the director suggested stone/bone against walnut) and oiled walnut.
   - No faux-wood print anywhere.
   - *Because* real wood makes glossy cream look like plastic. Matte bone looks like a deliberate partner, as plaster does next to oak.
4. **Lighter than it looks.** 6 mm of walnut (≈ 25–30 g, systems ESTIMATE) on a hollow shell. It should surprise in the hand.
5. **It lives on the wall.** Tree side up, like a small shelf.

## Look
- **Shell:** v1.1 ONE SHEET in matte bone, two finishes (smooth, rings in #c9bfac), v1.1 grooves and type, edge A keys.
  - The MIXER heading sits at y 136 (common §2 fix 3).
- **Walnut top:** two leaves, resawn from one offcut and end-flipped, so the live edge mirrors about x 138 (end match).
  - Oiled. A pale sapwood band (#b98f62) is kept along the edge. The top edge is eased by hand.
  - Overhang behind the printed rear wall: 0–12. I drew a gentle **crown on the axis** so POWER can be engraved behind the butterfly. With a real offcut, pick one whose edge crowns near the middle, or engrave POWER at y 178.
- **The butterfly:** **16 × 8** (I shrank it from 20 × 10 so it clears the PHONES legend by ≈ 1.6), bog oak / wenge, inlaid 3 mm across x 138, y 168.5–176.5.
- **Jack legends:** laser-engraved into the walnut (Inter Bold 1.5, dark), located from the straight joint (one fence). The ticks behind them point down to the ports.
- **FREE / SYNC:** v1.1 ONE SHEET style at (128, 132.7), "FREE" / "SYNC" beside it.
- **TIME:** two scales, marks only (as CHAMBER). TIME and DECAY legends behind.
- **Status light:** hooded.

## Motion and feedback
- As v1.1 ONE SHEET.
- The walnut is the one part you touch that isn't a control. Carrying it, your fingers land on the live edge. It should feel like a stair rail: oiled, warm, rounded by hand.

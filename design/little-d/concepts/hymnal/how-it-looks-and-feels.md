## The idea
The instrument in its own box: the cigar-box guitar's move, with the hymnal's habit of carrying what you'll play inside the cover.

## Principles
1. **The box is the protection.** *Because* a box needs no bag: closed, four walls and the lid enclose everything, keys included.
2. **It opens where the hands go.** *Because* a box that becomes an instrument must open fully at the front: the lid stands up as a desk, the front drops as a palm rest.
3. **The label is the identity.** *Because* cigar boxes are known by their labels: his own label outside (blank here), the patch chart inside the lid. No recall, so pencil.
4. **Old in the box, new on the panel.** *Because* old lettering on the panel would be a costume: walnut, cloth tape and paper outside; plain birch and white knobs inside.
5. **The key edge wears the edging.** *Because* the paper strip that binds a cigar box's edges also binds where the keys meet the box: flat, at panel height, with the key names.

## Look
- **Palette:**
  - oiled walnut;
  - cream paper edging on every top edge;
  - charcoal bookcloth hinges;
  - red only in the rules, numerals and patch chart;
  - birch panel engraved dark;
  - white KN1250 knobs; cream CUTOFF with a red line;
  - white and near-black acrylic short keys.
- **Type:** the panel is Inter (SemiBold legends 1.9 mm, Bold section names); the numerals are a bold serif in red; the patch chart is in Inter. The label lettering is his.
- **Silhouette, open:** lid up as a desk (≈ 104°, ribbon stay), front dropped flat. **Closed:** a plain walnut box with a blank label.

## Feel

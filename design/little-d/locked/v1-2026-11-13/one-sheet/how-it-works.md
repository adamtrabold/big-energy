## The idea
The economy concept: **the enclosure is as few prints as the printer allows, in one filament with one swap, and the toolpath is the finish** (the face is printed face down; the first-layer direction turns per section, so sections read by sheen at $0, visual round 3). Rev 4 adds the owner's unibody: **one shell, one face, opened only from behind.**

## Design principles (each follows from the idea)
1. **One face, split only where the panel already breaks.** One piece where a big enough bed exists; otherwise one joint at x = 122.5, the one break that runs through the whole panel, sitting at the bottom of the break groove. *Because* in an economy you don't add a line the drawing doesn't already have.
2. **The toolpath is the finish.** First-layer direction ±45° per section, rings under each knob (visual r3). *Because* the nozzle draws every line anyway; choosing its direction costs nothing. (Director: still needs the test coupon.)
3. **Face down on smooth steel.** *Because* the first layer is FDM's best surface, and it's where the legends and the finish happen.
4. **One filament, one swap.** Legends as cut-outs in the first 0.6 mm, filled dark by one change. *Because* no second process.
5. **Opened from behind.** Every fastener is underneath (one M3 size into brass inserts); the top carries only controls. *Because* the owner asked, and a kit that opens from the back keeps its face whole and reprintable.

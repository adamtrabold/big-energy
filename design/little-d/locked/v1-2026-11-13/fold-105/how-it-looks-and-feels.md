## Principles
1. **Block and pickguard.** One solid walnut body per half; one white plate carries everything, screwed from behind.
2. **The swap draws the pickguard's line.** White 1.0 mm, then black; a 45° bevel shows a white band over a black line on every edge.
3. **Three columns and one axis (NEW).** The owner felt it unbalanced. Systems mirrored every row; visually I made the datum visible. Random draw (real RNG): "Bridges — build / burn" + sailing rigging. *Build:* the column rules bridge the seam (one line from the base to the lid's back row). *Burn:* the signal map is gone, and so are the echo/reverb row-end words; nothing off-axis is left that pulls the eye sideways. *Rigging:* a boat's centreline is always marked; here a brass pin on the front rim and a tick on the far rim.
4. **Amounts under the hand, kinds behind.** Base = what you do during a note (incl. TAP, which mirrors the bend stick); the lid's back row runs the chain: drift → echo → reverb → volume.
5. **Open flat, closed a bar** (and now **standing at 105°**).

## Look
- **Blocks:** walnut-tone wood-fill PLA first (#6b4429, matte, fine speckle, no figure), his planed walnut later (oiled, figure). R 12 plan, R 6 outer face, R 3 meeting edges, flat top and bottom.
- **Pickguards:** cream (#f1ebdd) face, 45° bevel showing the 1.0 mm white over black.
- **Rules:** 1.2 mm sunk lines in a warm grey (#d3cab8): x 101.5 / 184.5 on the base from y 53.5 to the seam, on the lid from the seam to V2 + 9; back-row breaks x 94 / 143 / 192 (± 9 round the row).
- **Legends:** black lowercase Kalam Bold (Proof's hand), 2.35 under knobs; section names 2.4: pitch, filter, effects & out (base, y 85); oscillators, mixer, contours (lid, d V1 + 11.3, left / centre / right). All black, one swap (director ruling).
- **TYPE switches:** black 18T knobs; detents at −60 / −20 / +20 / +60°; the 4 names in Kalam 1.3 lowercase above, each over its detent, inner two lower, outer two higher (tape, bbd, clean, ping-pong / plate, hall, spring, shimmer).
- **TAP:** a cream 12 mm cap in a black 14 mm bezel, at the end of the base row (x 261).
- **Grille:** Proof's bars, 50 wide (x 118–168), centred on the axis; FOLD-105 d 28–46, FOLD-113 d 36–66.
- **Name:** a blank 32 × 6 lozenge, black outline, on the axis behind the back row.
- **Lid back:** 6 brass countersunk screws, 4 inlaid cork pads, 2 push-out holes.
- **Stays (NEW):** 3 × 8 brass flat bar, 52.4 long, ends R 4, brushed then waxed; pin = a domed brass rivet head, flush; in a 3.5 mm groove in each lid end face (d 30–82.4) with a 6 mm finger scoop at the free end. Black rubber tip, 9 Ø.

## Motion and feedback
- **Standing:** open past 120°, flip both stays out (a firm click at the hard stop), lower the lid onto them (a soft knock). Tip lands 54 mm behind the base. Flat and closed: stays flush, read as two brass lines.
- **Keys:** ≈ 3 mm straight down, silent switch.
- **TAP:** tempo-flash proposal on the status light (×4), as the flat concepts; needs systems/eng.

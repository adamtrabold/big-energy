## The idea, as it should read

The keyboard is its own lid (Polaroid SX-70). Played, it is one long flat slab
with the longest keys in the set (80 mm visible whites): the depth DOCK can't
avoid becomes its feel advantage. The joint is a visible, satisfying moment:
the module drops into its notch.

## Looks

- **Desk look:** smoked acrylic layers (edges glow faintly), black panel,
  green hero.
- **Body (300 × 136):** a row of vent slots over the D strip at the rear, the
  same panel as SLAB, and a wheel pod at the front-left **beside** the panel
  (my push: +15 mm width, panel untouched; systems put it inside 285 at the
  cost of the play group).
- **The joint:** seven small gold pads in the middle of the module's rear edge
  (pogo face) and two dark notches over the felt-faced cleats. They are the
  only "technical" marks, so they should look jewel-like, not industrial.
- **Module (300 × 100):** K-B long levers on Hall switches; wordmark at the
  rear-right of the module; magnet dots at the ends.

## Feel and motion, by state

| State | What you see / feel |
|---|---|
| carried | module face-up on the body, keys visible inside its rim; skirt hides the cap gap; ≈ 70 thick |
| lifting the module | body goes dark immediately (power-by-dock) |
| docking | module drops onto the cleats: a felt-damped seat, end magnets pull the last few mm; status LED breathes |
| playing | one flat slab, 33 mm; no tilt |
| MIDI owns a ringed control | one amber flash of the status LED, then nothing (V-b, the quiet option); HOLD + TUNE reclaims all |
| knocked dock | brown-out → reboot + re-tune (2–4 s): LED breathes again |
| lights off | glow pointers and sharp stripes, no pip (inset) |

## Why

- R3 asked for < 45 mm carried; systems shows the floor is 56 (eng stack +
  caps + module). I drew ≈ 70 rather than hide it.
- The wheels can't ride on the module while mod is an analog pot (eng §2). A
  pod beside the panel keeps every R1 minimum; if eng takes E1, the wheels move
  to the module and the body returns to 285.

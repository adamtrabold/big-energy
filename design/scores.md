### Rulings asked for

**R1. The lip rule (H3) is retired as a mechanical rule.** With bought Choc V2 switches, the lip no longer stops or carries the keys. "The lip is the spine" is no longer true, and keeping it would be decoration. It's replaced by **H3′, the key edge:**
- Wherever the keys meet the body, the edge comes from the concept's idea: rail, edging, skin or glass.
- It hides the switch housings and the back of strip C.
- It keeps the key tops from snagging in carry.
- A side section is still required, but now only as a fit check (heights, switch, top, magnet over a flat sensor). It's no longer the place the idea has to be argued.
- TYPE CASE (rail), HYMNAL (edging) and HUSH (felt deck as the bottom stop) already meet H3′. ONE SHEET's "lip as a key-name ruler" was the weakest answer: an engraved strip with no job.

**R2. Knob gaps: 4 mm is rejected.** A Tayda KN1250 (14.9 mm) at 19 mm pitch leaves 4.1 mm between knobs. You'll catch the neighbour on every turn, and the mocks look crowded (section IV most of all).
- **Keep the KN1250.** It's priced, it's stocked in colours, and it's the one family across the set.
- **Minimum left-right pitch is 20 mm** (5 mm gap) everywhere.
- **The played row in IV gets 24 mm** (≈ 9 mm gaps): CUTOFF, EMPHASIS, CONTOUR AMT.
- Take the width from the six 9 mm section gaps (down to 6 mm; TYPE CASE keeps 9 where it has walls).
- The front-back pitch (23 mm) is fine.
- If IV still won't fit, move the velocity toggle to the back row. Don't shrink the knob.

**R3. Thickness, against the owner's "~36 body / ≤ 40 to knob tops".** ONE SHEET (27) and HUSH (30) meet it. HYMNAL meets it: 37 box, 40 closed. TYPE CASE's 40 rim is a 40 body: inside the ceiling, but chunky (see its fixes). **All four assume eng's thin-speaker answer (E-L2).** Eng has to answer it before round 2, or every number moves by 3 mm.

## Settled (don't reopen)
- Read `PRINCIPLES.md` first: portable, harmonious proportion from the device's own ratio (not necessarily a rectangle), module plus a carried keyboard (any slim 25-key USB-C travel keyboard, not one model), panels make sense (synth → effects → output; related controls together), design from the idea of the thing, no decoration.
- **Case fully 3D printed, metal included (metal printing allowed); knobs and similar bought parts are the exception.** Cloth wrap only over a printed core. The bookcloth spine "feels weird" to him.
- **All controls in a single plane** (no keyboard on the module any more).
- **floss can sit at an angle.**
- The keyboard **doesn't have to be** permanently attached (it may be).
- Dimensions are free to change from CATHEDRAL and from the turned-down module.

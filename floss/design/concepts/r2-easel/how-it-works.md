## Rev 1 → rev 2, in one line
The driver leaves the plate for the foot, so the plate is only as long as two rows of controls. The body drops from 98.6 to ≈ 68 tall, and the empty upper field is gone.

## The idea
CATHEDRAL's raised lid, standing on its own. The section is now a **soft gable**: the plate at 60° in front, the back at 60° behind, on a low front face (the predella) that speaks through lancet slots. The end face reads as a cathedral's gable end, carrying the growth rings. One fixed arrangement, no moving parts.

## Fix 1: the driver in the foot, the plate sized to the controls
- **A Ø 55 driver firing straight out of the front face would need a front face ≥ ≈ 58 tall.** That's a taller object, the opposite of the fix [calc].
- **So the driver lies flat in the foot, cone down.** It's mounted on a horizontal baffle at z ≈ 10–12. Under the cone, a ≈ 6–8 mm front chamber opens forward through **lancet slots in the predella**: 8 slots × ≈ 8 × 12 ≈ 770 mm², above eng's ≥ 620.
- **The box is the volume above the baffle.** The driver fires at the player's hands and the table, which reflects it up.
- **UNVERIFIED (eng):** a slot-loaded front chamber acts as a low-pass, likely somewhere in the kHz range. It would dull the shimmer's top. This is the main acoustic risk of the fix.
- **Plate L ≈ 58**, set by two rows of controls: lower rim, rows at s 14 and 31 along the slope (knob A pitch), a band of lights at s ≈ 45, upper rim. That's the director's ≈ 55–60.

## Fix 2: the section and the ratio, restated
- **Front face (predella) f = 18:** slots in the centre, host USB-C to the left of them, facing the keyboard.
- **Plate:** 60° (CATHEDRAL's raised lid), L ≈ 58.
- **Back:** also 60°, meeting the plate at a soft apex ≈ 68 up.
- **Footprint F ≈ 68:** a Ø 55 frame lying flat needs ≥ ≈ 62 inside. The magnet (Ø ≈ 25, UNVERIFIED) needs ≈ 40 of height above the floor at its rear edge, under the 60° back, which pushes F to 66–68.
- **The 1 : 1.618 ratio no longer fits.** With the driver in the foot, the foot is set by the driver (≈ 68). φ would then make it 110 tall, worse than rev 1.
- **The deliberate ratio becomes the square:** **as tall as it is deep (68 × 68)**, both faces at 60°. It's an equilateral gable, cut off at the front by the predella.

## Fix 3: r 17.5 plan corners
- They work, but they shape the end face.
- **Where the end face fits:** two 17.5 corners fit only where the section is ≥ 35 deep, so **below z ≈ 37**. Above that, the corners meet and **the apex ends round off** like a hipped roof. That's soft, and it's the director's call whether it reads as CATHEDRAL.
- **What's left flat:** a small gable on each end, ≈ 33 wide at the base, ≈ 37 tall. Enough for the port mouth (Ø 12 + a narrow flare) at its centre, and for the rings.
- **What doesn't fit:** **the jack row** (≈ 60 of width), hence the back face.
- **Fallback:** r 17.5 at the front corners (the plate, the family face) and r 8 at the back.

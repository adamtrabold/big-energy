## The idea, as you see it
CATHEDRAL's raised lid, standing on its own. From the end it's a tall soft triangle with a vertical back and a short flat roof where the point would be. From the front it's a framed cream panel leaning back at 60°, with nothing propping it. Of the four, it's the most recognisable at thumbnail size (sheet view 7).

**Version A** (footprint 50, a sharp 30° top) is dashed on the end view. It's narrower, but its top can't be softened: an r 8 round loses ≈ 23 mm of height.

## Feel
- **On a café table:** a small lectern, 61 deep, the keyboard tucked right in front. The panel faces you like a music stand.
- **Hands:** the knobs sit low, near the wrist. Above them the plate is quiet and carries only the lights, which keeps the face calm.
- **Touching it:** rest a palm on the roof and it steadies. A light pull at the roof tips it forward, so you pick it up low, by the ends.
- **In the bag:** a 1.07 L prism that stands on its base, but the panel faces out.

## Costs, stated plainly (they're on the comparison sheet too)
- Controls only in the lower 45 mm of the plate.
- A pull of ≈ 1.5–2 N at the top tips it forward.
- Four boards instead of two.
- The weakest panel protection of the four (the rim is only 1 mm over the knobs, and the panel faces out when packed).
- Not flat or slightly angled: it's steep, because that's what he asked for at 20:46. Job 32 (rev 6) is unsupported until he rules on scope.

# CASKET-B: how it works (systems, idea level)

2026-11-22. PROPOSAL. Rulings: director turn 5 (B, ≈ 11°, closes as a calm slab; A dropped; TRIPTYCH one line). Sizes are [calc] from `eng/module-r3.md` facts. Nothing is measured.

## The idea
Closed, floss is CATHEDRAL closed: a calm walnut slab with his pipe façade on the lid. Open, the lid goes under the module's back and sets the angle by its own depth. With the lid off entirely, floss plays flat. **The only concept that is flat *and* angled with no moving parts.**

## Structure
- **Module:** the eng-proven slab, 280 × 105–115 × 35 [eng]. Rim 1 mm over the knob tops, flat base, everything inside as r3 with speaker E.
- **Lid:** a printed shallow tray, ≈ 15 deep (skin plus a ≈ 10 skirt over the rim, which already clears the knobs). The pipe façade is on its outside.
- **Underside of the module: two recesses, placed so they never clash.** Both are recesses, so the base stays flat for the flat pose.

| Recess | Where (y from the front edge) | Job |
|---|---|---|
| SHOE dock (S9) | y ≈ 10–70, centred, entered from the front | keyboard carry (`../c-shoe.md`) |
| **Catch groove** | y ≈ 80–92, full width | the lid's skirt edge drops into it, so the module can't slide off the lid |

- **10 mm between them.** The front 10 mm stays solid as the front foot.
- **Angle:** θ = asin(lid depth / contact distance) = asin(15 / ≈ 80) ≈ **11°**. The groove sits just behind the module's centre of mass, so the support spans it: front edge to groove. PROPOSAL: eng's centre of mass fixes the groove's exact y.

## Poses
| Pose | How | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Closed** | lid over the plate | the slab, ≈ 50 thick; the façade on top |
| **Flat** | lid off, set aside or under the bag; module on its base | 0° |
| **Angled** | lid face down, skirt edge in the catch groove, module's front edge on the table | ≈ 11° |

- **Where the lid lies [calc]:** its front skirt wall sits in the groove, so the lid runs **backward** from y ≈ 80 and shows ≈ 75–80 mm behind the module's rear edge. Played angled, the footprint is ≈ 190 deep. The lid can't go further forward, because then the module's front edge couldn't reach the table. **Visual and director:** this is the part of the angled pose that could read as "a box on a tray". Seen from the player it's hidden behind the module.
- The façade faces the table while playing angled. That's fine: it's the closed face (director §5).
- **Stability:** support from the front foot to the groove, ≈ 80 deep and 280 wide. A knob press and the hand on the front edge stay inside it.
- **Lap and grass:** flat pose like any slab. Angled, the lid's edge and the front foot sit on two lines, which is firmer on grass than on a curved lap (UNVERIFIED feel).

## Speaker and ports
- **Speaker:** E's two drivers under a lancet grille in the plate, as CATHEDRAL's lid had it.
- **Port:** exits the left end face, centred in the growth rings (the family rule).
- **USB-C host:** on the front face, left of centre, horizontal in both poses. A straight plug clears at ≈ 17 up.
- **Jacks, power, device USB-C, TUNE, MIDI SPLIT:** on the rear face. Angled, the rear is ≈ 15–50 up; flat, 0–35. Reachable in every pose (job 34).

## Moves: bag to sound
| | Moves |
|---|---|
| Angled | out; slide the keyboard off; lid off; lid face down where the back goes; set the module on it: **5** |
| Flat | out; keyboard off; lid off; set down: **4** |

**Packing:** reverse. The closed slab is ≈ 280 × 107–117 × 50, plus ≈ 15–20 with the keyboard docked under it. It's the calmest of the three in the bag. The cable and adapter go in a pocket in the lid's skirt corner (UNVERIFIED that they fit in ≈ 10 mm).

**Or hinged, so the lid never leaves (one moving part): TRIPTYCH.** That's 3–4 moves, but there's a hinge to make.

## States
- **Lid lost or forgotten:** it plays flat; the angle needs the lid (or a book under the back).
- **No keyboard:** plays from USB or TRS.
- **Battery and status:** unchanged.

## Owner forks
- **Flat too:** yes → CASKET-B is the candidate; no → GNOMON or DROP.
- **Carry:** SHOE / loose / TPU band.
- **Cast metal:** port ring or none.

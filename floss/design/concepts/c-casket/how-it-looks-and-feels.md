# CASKET-B: how it looks and feels (visual, idea level)

2026-11-22. PROPOSAL. Read with `how-it-works.md` (systems): module 280 × 107 × 35; lid ≈ 15 deep; angled on the lid's skirt in the catch groove; closed ≈ 50. Picture: `sheet.png`. Source: `../_src/cathedral_sheets.py`.

## The idea, as you see it
**Closed, floss is CATHEDRAL closed:** one calm walnut slab with soft corners and his pipe façade on top. That's the family face, and the reason this concept exists.

**Open:** the lid goes face down under the module's back, and its own depth sets a low desk angle. **Lid off:** it plays flat. No moving parts, two pieces.

## Why the shape (not costume)
- The slab is the eng-proven module. The lid is only as deep as the rim needs to clear the knobs, and that depth *is* the angle.
- Every surface has a job: the lid protects the plate, carries the façade, and is the stand.
- **Drawn honestly:** in the angled pose the lid lies behind and shows ≈ 80 mm past the module's back (footprint ≈ 190 deep). From the player's seat it's hidden behind the module. From the side it's the part that could read as "a box on a tray", so both sheet views show it plainly.
- **The lid is the same walnut as the module**, with a seam line, not a darker tone. Closed, it has to read as one slab, as CATHEDRAL closed did.
- I drew the angle from the numbers (asin 15 / 86): **≈ 10°**. Systems' file says ≈ 11°. It depends on where eng's centre of mass puts the groove.

## The underside (drawn once on this sheet, shared)
- **SHOE dock:** y ≈ 10–70, slides in from the front. It's the same on all three modules.
- **Lid catch groove:** y ≈ 80–92, full width (CASKET only).
- Both are recesses, so the base stays flat for the flat pose.

## Materials (all printed except the knobs)
- **Module and lid:** printed walnut-brown.
- **Plate:** printed cream.
- **Façade:** printed in relief, or with printed-metal line work if cast counts.
- **Grip strip:** printed TPU at the front edge.
- **Knobs:** bought.
- **No cloth spine.**

## Feel
- **Closed:** the object you'd leave on a shelf. Quiet, square-shouldered, his façade on top.
- **Opening is a small ritual:** lift the lid, turn it over, set the module's back in the groove. That's 5 moves from bag to sound, the most of the three. It's the price of flat-and-angled and full protection.
- **Playing angled:** a low desk synth, the plate barely tipped toward you.
- **In the bag:** the calmest of the three, ≈ 50 thick plus the keyboard on the SHOE, or loose.
- **Or hinged, so the lid never leaves (one moving part): TRIPTYCH.**

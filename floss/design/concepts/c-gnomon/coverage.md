## Verdict
**Every job is supported. None is unsupported, so nothing sends GNOMON back.** Eleven jobs carry stated weaknesses (3, 6, 18, 24, 26, 28, 29, 31, 32, 33, 34). Each is named in the concept files or here, and none stops a job being done.

## For the handoff (not blockers)
1. Host port low on a cheek near the rear-bottom corner (director a). **The port light stays on the plate.** Check clearance from the port mouth (left) or the jacks (right).
2. Cells and E's box low and away from the plate, for upright resistance (director b).
3. One jack row on ≈ 12 mm pitch above the end cell: UNVERIFIED (director c).
4. Undock the keyboard before standing it upright (director d).
5. Write the battery, flash and host-port access through the slab floor (job 26).
6. The split target rail on the front face below the r16 lip (job 6).

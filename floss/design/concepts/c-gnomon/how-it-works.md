# GNOMON: how it works (systems, idea level)

2026-11-22. PROPOSAL (the director's idea, turn 3). Rulings: turn 5 (sin θ = T / D accepted; no façade on the underside). Sizes are [calc] from `eng/module-r3.md` facts. Nothing is measured.

## The idea
CATHEDRAL's lid standing on its own ends. A framed slab of constant thickness, the slab eng already proved holds everything. At each end the rim carries on down into a wedge-shaped **cheek**. The back lifts by the slab's own thickness, so **sin θ = T / D**: the angle is the slab's latitude.

## Structure, from the end face
- **Slab:** T = 35, D = 105–115 [eng]. It holds the plate, the board, the cells at the ends, and speaker E's box in the rear region, unchanged from r3.
- **Cheeks:** the rim continues down at both ends into a triangle. Their bottom edges are the **runners**, with TPU grip strips.
- **θ ≈ 19.5° (D 105) / 17.7° (D 115).** Whatever eng's D comes out at, nobody rounds it.
- **The back stands at ≈ 70 = 2T**: the end face is the slab plus its own thickness again.
- **Footprint ≈ 280 × 99–110.**
- **Underside:** open between the cheeks. No façade there (director §5).

## What lives where
| Volume / surface | Job |
|---|---|
| Slab | everything electronic and acoustic (as r3, speaker E) |
| Cheeks | the stand; the left cheek carries the port mouth |
| Hollow under the slab | **the cable bay when packed** (cable and C-to-A adapter in a printed clip). Playing, it's only the space the tilt makes |
| Stretcher (**only with SHOE**) | a printed bar between the cheeks at table level, y ≈ 20–80. It holds the S9 dock, stiffens the cheeks against racking, and floors the cable bay. **Director check:** it changes "open underneath"; without SHOE there's no stretcher |

## Speaker and ports
- **Speaker:** E's two drivers under a **lancet grille in the plate**, as CATHEDRAL's lid had it.
- **Port:** Ø 16 × 140 along x, out through the **left cheek face, at the centre of its growth rings**. Not into the hollow, which a lap or turf half-closes (UNVERIFIED that it would choke, but avoided).
- **USB-C host:** on the slab's front face, left of centre. That face leans 19° forward, so a straight plug clears the table by only ≈ 6.6. The kit's right-angle ("upwards") cable lies flat along the face.
- **Jacks, power, device USB-C, TUNE, MIDI SPLIT:** on the **rear face**, raised ≈ 35–70 off the table and facing back and up. The easiest reach of the three.

## Poses
- **One pose, at θ.**
- **Hand on the front edge:** it pushes into the support line. Stable.
- **Knob press:** for every row, the line of force meets the table inside the footprint.
- **Lap:** the runners land one per thigh, like a lap desk. The best lap answer of the three.
- **Grass:** ≈ 3.8 kPa on the runners [ESTIMATE 0.8 kg]. It sinks evenly and the angle holds.
- **Sliding:** the TPU strips grip; brass only at the runner ends, and only if cast counts.
- **If the owner wants flat too:** GNOMON can't. Angled only.

## Moves: bag to sound (3)
1. Out of the bag.
2. Slide the keyboard off (or take it from the bag).
3. Set down, plug in (the cable is in the bay), switch on.

**Packing:** a wedge, 280 × ≈ 100–110 × 70; ≈ 81–86 with the keyboard docked. The docked keyboard closes the cable bay.

## States
- **No keyboard:** plays from USB or TRS.
- **Loose carry:** the cable bay is open; a printed clip still holds the cable.
- **Battery and status:** unchanged.

## Making
The slab prints as r3 (one piece on a ≥ 300 bed, or three pieces split at the dividers, or NAVE bays with seams as engraved hairlines). Each cheek prints with its end cap. The stretcher is one flat print.

## Owner forks
- **Flat too:** no → GNOMON is a candidate; yes → CASKET-B.
- **Carry:** SHOE (with the stretcher) / loose / TPU band.
- **Cast metal:** runner ends and the port ring, or none.

## UNVERIFIED (eng)
- the port through a cheek wall;
- the stretcher's need or stiffness;
- the right-angle cable's length (eng found 0.2 / 1 m only);
- the centre of mass with E's box at the rear.

The module is SILL's slab with no recess: 280 × 115 × 35, speaker E, ports as GNOMON rev 5. [calc] = my arithmetic; mass ≈ 0.8 kg is an ESTIMATE; UNVERIFIED = eng or a trial.

## The idea
CATHEDRAL's lid and base, as two pieces. The module plays **flat on its own**. Docked on a separate printed wedge, it plays **slightly angled**. The wedge sits **set in from the slab's outline**, with a dark gap line, so the soft slab seems to float over a smaller base. Nothing moves except the cable drawer.

## Structure
- **Module:** unchanged, with the S9 dock in its base (y 10–70, as SILL; nothing recessed into E's rear box). So **D 105 is possible again** (eng's 4 % margin). It's drawn at 115 to match SILL.
- **Plinth:** printed, plan **260 × 95** (the slab inset 10 all round), n 5 corners concentric with the slab's.
  - **Front height T/3 ≈ 11.7, rear ≈ 26.3.**
  - **Shadow gap:** the plinth's top drops ≈ 3 mm in a rebate round its outer ≈ 6 mm. So besides the 10 mm overhang, a dark 3 mm line separates the slab from the plinth in every view. Visual sets the depths. Structurally, any overhang ≤ ≈ 15 is fine.
  - **Top:** an S9 tongue (the SHOE plate's dovetail, 200 × 60) at plinth y 0–60, under the module's dock.
  - **Underside:** an S9 dock, so SHOE docks the keyboard under the plinth.
  - **Drawer:** slides out of the **right end**, ≈ 120 × 80 inside, 7.7 (front) to 22 (rear) clear. It holds the 0.3–0.5 m C-to-C coiled (≈ Ø 50–60 × 8), the C-to-A adapter and the card. A printed bump catch holds it shut (no load on it).

## The angle, from a part
The plinth tilts the slab so its back stands **T/2 higher than its front**, as SILL and CRADLE do: **θ = asin(T/2 ÷ D) ≈ 8.75°** [calc]. Plinth rise over its 95 depth = 95 tan θ ≈ 14.6, so 11.7 → 26.3.

**Angled sizes [calc]:** the slab base runs from z ≈ 10 (front) to ≈ 28 (back). Top front lip ≈ 45, back ≈ 62. Footprint 260 × 95; overall ≈ 280 × 120.

## How it attaches
- **Dock:** set the module on the plinth ≈ 60 behind its place and slide it **toward you, downhill**, until it clicks.
- **The slope does the holding:** gravity pulls it **into** the dovetail's closed end (W sin θ ≈ 1.2 N). Knob presses push the same way. The detent only matters in the bag.
- **Undock:** slide it away from you and lift. One move.
- **"Japanese joinery" check:** S9 is a sliding dovetail (ari-zashi). Nothing new is needed.

## Stability [calc]
- **Table, angled:** footprint 260 × 95. A wrist on the front lip tips it at ≈ 36 N. End knobs sit ≈ 14 inside the plinth's ends.
- **Flat:** whole base.
- **Lap:** the wedge sits broad on both thighs; flat works too.
- **Grass:** trivial pressure. Nothing tips.

## Packed
- **Bundle:** a wedge ≈ 280 × 120 × 45 (front) / 62 (back) ≈ 1.8 L. With a slim keyboard docked under the plinth, ≈ 58–63 / 75–80 tall, and the keyboard overhangs the ends as on SHOE.
- **Apart:** the slab 280 × 115 × 35 + the plinth 260 × 95 × 26.
- It's a wedge whichever way it packs. Two parts of one shape can't nest into a rectangle unless a second wedge is added (not proposed).

## Ports and speaker
- **Unchanged from GNOMON rev 5:** host on the front face (≈ 10 above the table when angled; a straight plug fits), jacks one row on the right end, port mouth on the left end, drivers in the plate. All are open in both states, and **no face of the module is a foot** except its base.
- **End speakers (asked):** unaffected. The ends overhang the plinth.

## Family
The plinth is an S9 part on both faces (a tongue on top, a dock below). **One plinth design per module plan, all on the same interface**, so it becomes the family's "base" joining any module to any keyboard. That's the strongest job 25 / 28 story of the three.

## Making and weight
- **One print, no metal, no pin, no spring.** At 45° it fits a 256 bed (diagonal span ≈ 251) [calc]. The drawer is one more print.
- **Plinth ≈ 0.14–0.17 kg** (ASA, 1.6–2.0 walls) [est]. Kit ≈ 0.84–1.02 kg.
- **Named trial:** the S9 fit (shared with SHOE).

## The idea, as you see it
CATHEDRAL's lid and base, as two pieces. On the plinth, the soft walnut slab **floats** over a smaller, **darker** wedge: set in 10 mm all round, with a 3 mm shadow gap where the plinth's top steps down round its edge. That's the one move of form. Off the plinth it's simply the module.

## Feel
- **Docking:** set it on the plinth and slide it toward you, downhill. It seats with a soft dovetail click and gravity keeps it there.
- **Playing:** the darkest thing on the table is under the instrument, so the slab looks lifted.
- **Flat:** just the slab. The plinth sits beside it as the cable's home.
- **In the bag:** it packs docked, so out of the bag it's already angled: two moves to sound.

## Honest about distinctiveness
Tilted, it passes the thumbnail test (a slab over a smaller dark wedge). **Flat, it doesn't:** it's SILL's rounded block, because it *is* the bare module. No surface treatment on the module fixes that without decoration.

# HANDLE: how it works (systems, idea level) — KILLED

2026-11-22, round 2, turn 4. The director's condition (turn 3 amendment): "Kill if it's neither simple nor distinctive." **It's neither, so it's killed.** No coverage file. [calc] from the same slab (280 × 115 × 35, mass ≈ 0.8 kg ESTIMATE).

## The idea tested
A U-shaped bail lies flush in a groove round the base's rear and ends, pivoting near the front of each end. Flush = flat; swung down past the base = props the back; swung up = a carry handle.

## Why it can't be simple [calc]
1. **A ≈ 9° prop from a long bail is a shallow lever.** With the arms ≈ 100 long, the bail stops only ≈ 14° below the base. The prop's load (≈ 4 N, plus ≈ 11 N from a resting palm) acts ≈ 97 mm from the pivot, so ≈ **1.4 N·m** tries to fold the bail back up. A detent at r 6 would need ≈ 240 N. It needs a **spring-loaded toothed hub with a release** on each side: two springs, two clutches and a button. SILL avoids this because its bar stands at ≈ 90°, so the load runs along the bar into the stop.
2. **The carry and the stand want different pivots.**
   - Pivots near the front (the stand) make it hang nose-down, because the centre of mass is ≈ 57 back.
   - Balanced pivots at mid-end shorten the arms to ≈ 58. The stop goes to ≈ 24°, and it's still a lever needing a clutch.
3. **Material:** a printed ASA U, 8 × 8, carrying 0.8 kg with bag jolts (× 3) runs at ≈ 20 MPa at mid-span and creeps over time. It would have to be printed metal.
4. **Groove:** 8 × 8 round the rear and ends ≈ **28 cm³** out of E's box, as much as SILL's recess. The arms also run along the end band, just under the jacks (right) and the port mouth (left). That fits, ≈ 5 mm clear, but crowded.

## Why it isn't distinctive
At rest the bail is a line in a groove: **its end view is SILL's rectangle**, flat and angled alike (angled, there's only a thin leg behind). It fails the thumbnail test in both playing states. Only the carry pose is distinctive, and that's not a playing pose.

## What survives
The "visible line" idea is already on SILL's sheet as the optional bronze bar (director). A carry handle, if he ever wants one, is a separate question from the stand.

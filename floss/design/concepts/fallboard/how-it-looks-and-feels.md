Status: **rev 3, 2026-11-22**, product designer (visual focus), visual turn 3: **recoloured to the walnut-brown house** (director steer 2, S3) and the lid drawn **ribbed on a 17.5 grid** (eng `module-r3.md` §6c); everything else as rev 2 (concept round 2, visual turn 2, printed case, the director's R6). **Rev 1's walnut tray, bookcloth bed, brass scale, catch and stay, the gutter and the lamp are withdrawn.** The module's look is in `../module/how-it-looks-and-feels.md` (drawn here with knob option A); systems' `how-it-works.md` rev 2 sets the structure.

## 1. Point of view
**A piano's fallboard over a borrowed keyboard, printed in one walnut-brown.** One shallow tray, one plane, one lid. The guest keyboard lies against the front wall; floss sits behind it like a nameboard, its cream stage the only lit surface in a brown frame. The tray, lid and module are one house colour, so the kit reads as one piece of furniture with a borrowed keyboard in it.

## 2. Materials and colour (the family system: see `../module/how-it-looks-and-feels.md` §1)
| Part | Finish |
|---|---|
| Tray (four printed quarters), lid (four quarters), end blocks, brace, hinges | **walnut-brown ASA (or PETG)**, one colour throughout, the module's filament. Bed, lane and pocket floors are the same brown (director R6.3): pockets show by their walls |
| Wall tops | the same brown; they catch light, so in plan they read a shade lighter than the floors |
| Printed scale (0–32 from each end, on the front lip) and an axis tick | **cream** marks (second spool), or debossed 0.6 mm on a one-spool build |
| End blocks | the same brown (drawn a shade lighter: they stand proud); faces of bought black closed-cell foam; black thumbscrews |
| Print seams | on the axis and on the lane's centre line (systems §8). **Left as honest lines**, and on the lid made deliberate: a 1 mm V-groove cross, printed into a one-piece lid too (director S5 accepted the four-panel lid) |
| Lid ribs | underneath, on a **17.5 mm grid** with a **1.2 mm skin** (eng r3 §6c: halves the sag, stops oil-canning; +≈ 30–60 g). On the closed sheet they're ghosted as a faint grid; on a 1.2 skin they may show faintly through in raking light, which is acceptable as the lid's only texture |
| Name | **none on the carry.** The wordmark is on the module (crown rear face), seen through the rear wall's centre between the windows when the module's out |

No coral on the kit: the closed object is plain brown. Coral lives on the module (DEPTH).

## 4. Packing (sheet B)
1. Switch off at the module's rear facet, through the left window.
2. Unplug the keyboard end; the tail runs along the lane; coil it and the adapter into the left pocket (the cable stays in the host port).
3. Thumb-lock both end blocks.
4. Lid down: the foam strip presses the keyboard; the knobs sit under the module's rim.
5. Magnets catch. Into the pack on its 265 edge. Charger and card ride in the right pocket.

## 5. Size and weight (sheet B)
- **Closed 412 × 265 × 42** (knob option B: 48, and 412 × 272). About a 15-inch laptop's footprint, 2½ times as thick.
- **Weight** (systems [calc/est] from eng [r2]; lighter is better, no target): carry 0.47–0.60 kg; kit + nanoKEY2 1.42–1.81, + K-Board C 1.52–1.91, + Xkey 25 1.78–2.17 kg. **Still the heaviest kit**; printing removed the bookcloth-lid lever, so the gain over rev 1 is modest. Said plainly on the sheet.

## 6. How it compares with YOKE (now a concept, steer 2 S1)
The owner's correction (the carry "does not need to surround it, just have an attachment point") makes FALLBOARD's tray an assumption, not a requirement. Against YOKE (`../yoke/`, systems rev 1: 0.14–0.20 kg carry, 395 × 135 × 74 ≈ 3.9 L): FALLBOARD protects everything, gives the best bench pose (the lid as stand and shade) and a home for every loose part, but costs ≈ 0.3–0.45 kg more carry and ≈ 0.7 L more volume. If the owner reads "attachment point" as a preference, FALLBOARD is the most "surrounding" kit and the most exposed to it.

## 7. Why
- **One plane, one brown:** the tray fixes the mapping and the cable path; the brown frame makes the kit one piece of furniture and puts the cream stage forward.
- **Keyboard against the front wall:** every keyboard packs to one datum, and the leftover depth falls behind it, where the plug is (every area one job, R6.2).
- **The lid earns its place twice:** protection and a stand/shade.
- **Seams left as lines, no name:** an honest printed object; floss's name is on floss.

## The idea
**Life lives in time.** It's HUSH's felt pebble: a soft grey stone you set on a bench, plant a chord in, and tend. The coral DRIFT is the only big thing on its face. HOLD is a 26th maple peg. Warm light breathes out of the speaker holes as the chord wanders.

**Draws on:** TIDE (systems turn 4, director turn 5), "listen to the quiet voice" (director's draw), the lantern (turn 2, lighthouse optics; three phases verified by systems), HUSH (felt, pegs, pressed words, tags), and BOW's panel grammar (amounts in front, ranges and times behind).

## Principles
1. **A stone you set down and tend.** *Because* TIDE is played less than it's looked after. It's soft and heavy-feeling, nothing hard touches the hand but what you touch, and every word is pressed blind into the felt.
2. **HOLD is a key.** *Because* in TIDE every note you play joins the chord, so HOLD is a maple peg on its own felt collar, on the axis, nearest the keys.
3. **Coral is drift.** *Because* the life is in how the notes wander. One large coral knob, on the axis.
4. **The light comes out where the sound does.** *Because* the lantern follows the ensemble's three phases through the speaker holes. It never shows state. Amber means analog, paler means a Daisy mode.
5. **Same grammar as BOW.** *Because* the family should read alike: amounts in front, ranges and times behind, octaves at the outer ends.

## Look
- **Body:** HUSH's printed pebble, 290 × 186 × 25.5, rear corners R 44, front corners R 16 in plan, a top round-over of r 9 so the felt can be blocked over it.
- **Felt:** one skin of mid-grey undyed wool (#9b968d drawn). It ends only underneath.
  - Collars a shade darker (#827d75).
  - Words pressed with a hot brass die, so they read darker (compressed fibre).
- **Tags:** light warm grey print (#d8d3c9), flush in the felt, under every knob (HUSH). The tick rings print on the tags.
- **Keys:** HUSH's pale maple pegs, Ø 12.7; sharps a darker cut (#b98f55). Whites at y 20, sharps at y 43, each on a felt collar.
- **HOLD:** a Ø 16 maple peg on a Ø 20 collar at (145, 70), push-push, so it stays down while on.
- **DRIFT:** Ø 24, coral (part open), at (145, 106). The status light (a small green LED) is on the axis behind it at y 134.
- **Knobs:** cream KN1250 Ø 14.9 on tags; octaves the same part, at the ends.
- **TAP:** a dark cap in a dark bezel, front right (it's not the status light here).
- **Speaker and lantern:** a punched field of Ø 1.7 holes at a 3.2 / 3.0 pitch (hex), ≈ 84 × 30 mm, centred on the axis at y 156, over the driver.
  - Three warm LEDs sit behind it, one per ensemble phase.
  - In the drawing, two warm pools drift across the field. That's one frame of a slow movement.

## Motion and feedback
- **Setting it down:** rear switch on. The status light goes steady, and the lantern comes up to a dim, still glow over about 2 s, like a lamp warming.
- **Planting** (systems' F8):
  1. Press the HOLD peg; it stays down.
  2. Play a soft note: it blooms slowly.
  3. Lean on another: it gets its own wider wander, remembered once held.
- **Tending:** hands off the keys. Turn DRIFT and the chord starts to beat against itself. Raise depth and the light starts to move: three pools sliding across the holes at the ensemble's ≈ 1 Hz, with a faint shimmer at ≈ 6 Hz. Depth 0 leaves the glow still. A Daisy mode turns the light paler.
- **Pulling a note out:** strike a held key again. You hear it leave; nothing lights.
- **HOLD off:** press the peg again; it rises, and everything fades on its own tail.

## States (visual, beyond common)
| State | What you see |
|---|---|
| on, idle | light steady; lantern dim and still |
| shimmer analog, depth up | amber pools moving through the holes |
| Daisy mode | paler pools |
| depth 0 | dim, still glow |
| HOLD on | peg down (by eye and by finger) |
| which notes are held | **not shown** (no screen); you hear them. Systems named this gap honestly |
| low battery during a long hold | the status light flashes. The lantern doesn't change, because it never signals state |

## Push, reshape, risks
- **Push:** TIDE is the most distinctive face of the three, and the one a stranger in the park would ask about. The lantern only makes sense here.
- **An honest visual weakness:** the back third of the pebble (y 120–186) is mostly speaker field and empty felt. HUSH was that deep because of its board. If eng's board for TIDE is smaller, the pebble should get shallower (≈ 160), not filled with more controls. That empty felt is part of "nothing shouts".
- **Risk:** sun. Felt and a warm lantern are a dusk-and-shade object. It still plays and reads by position in sun, but it looks its best in shade. That's fine for an inferred job, and worth saying.
- **Risk:** DRIFT at Ø 24 needs a real large knob in coral (part open). Fallback: a cream Ø 24 knob with a coral cap insert.
- **Open:**
  - the HOLD peg's mechanism (a push-push switch under a peg; eng);
  - the radiator's direction and the foot ring;
  - whether the lantern shows through 1.7 mm holes in felt in shade (a test swatch).

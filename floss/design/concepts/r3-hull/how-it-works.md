# HULL: how it works (systems, idea level) — **KILLED**

2026-11-23, round 3. The director's idea: a rockered hull with two flat facets ≈ 9.5° apart, flat on one and slightly angled on the other. One piece, zero parts. The director's kill rule: "If it rocks under a top-row press, or needs a detent, kill it and say why." **It does both.** [calc; mass 0.8 kg and COM ESTIMATES; a SILL-sized slab, D 105, T 35]

## Why two facets can't both hold
- **The geometry:** the centre of mass has to sit over facet 1 when it rests on facet 1, and over facet 2 after rolling 9.5°.
  - With the facets meeting at a ridge, that only works if the COM sits ahead of the ridge by less than **h · tan 9.5°**, where h is the COM's height above the facets.
  - A rocker arc between the facets makes it worse, not better: the arc's centre is higher, so the window shrinks.
- **The window:**
  - h ≈ 17 (slab alone) gives a **≈ 2.8 mm** window.
  - A hull 10 deep under the slab gives h ≈ 27 and ≈ 4.5 mm. This is the knife edge found in the jam's turn 1.
- **The restoring moment,** COM centred in the window: ≈ 8 N × 2.2 mm ≈ **17 N·mm in each pose**.

## What that means under the hand
The COM sits about mid-slab (≈ 52 from the front), so the ridge has to sit there too. Both knob rows (t ≈ 22 / 42) are then ahead of it.

| Pose | Load | Rocks it at |
|---|---|---|
| Angled (on facet 2) | **lower-row press, ≈ 30 ahead of the ridge** | **≈ 0.6 N** |
| Angled | upper-row press, ≈ 10 ahead | ≈ 1.8 N |
| Angled | palm on the front edge, ≈ 52 ahead | ≈ 0.3 N |
| Flat (on facet 1) | press on the rear band (utilities / grille), behind the ridge | ≈ 0.6 N |

- A knob turn or a resting hand flips it between poses. Holding a pose needs a click-stop or a magnet, which is a detent (killed by the rule).
- **Moving the COM doesn't save it.** The rows can only be over the stable side of the ridge if the COM moves ≈ 40 mm forward. Even the cells (≈ 90 g) moved to the front edge shift it only ≈ 10.
- **Bigger facet angles don't help either:** the window grows with tan φ, but he asked for a slight angle.

## What survives from it
- The glaze-line idea (finished top over a plain foot, the shell's split line as the one line) is a visual idea any concept can use.
- A hull with a **single** flat facet plus a fixed slight angle is possible, but that's LOW GNOMON's job (already replaced).

## Verdict
**Kill.** The two-facet hull rocks at ≈ 0.3–1.8 N, a light touch, so it needs a detent. That's physics, not drawing.

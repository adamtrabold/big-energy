# CLOTH: how it works (systems)

Status: **rev 1, 2026-11-22**, product designer (systems focus), concept round 2. The one module (297 × 105 × 40 at the rim) is in `../module/how-it-works.md`; this file covers only the carry. Marks: **[r2]** · **[eng]** · **[calc]** · **PROPOSAL** · **UNVERIFIED**.

**The idea:** two objects in a soft frame, unpacked in a fixed order like a tea set. The module and keyboard travel wrapped in one quilted square cloth (a furoshiki, the traditional Japanese wrapping cloth). Spread out, it's the playing surface and the frame: it owns the outline and hosts the keys.
- It gains most from the owner's 13:25 and 13:29 direction: its module is now simply *the* module, about A4 long.
- **Honest label:** a bench-and-table kit (director, turn 6).

**Where the module sits, and why:**
- **Open:** behind the keyboard on the outline, centred, the same relation as the rigid kits. So the mapping and the cable path are the same.
- **Packed:**
  1. module in its sleeve at the bottom, panel up;
  2. the keyboard keys-down on the sleeve's quilted top;
  3. the cloth knotted over both.
- **Why this order:** the keys bear only the keyboard's own weight (0.24–0.6 kg [eng]), on quilt over the module's flat rim. The knobs sit under the rim.

**Every square centimetre (spread 800 × 800):** the outline (1,187 cm²) holds the module and keyboard. The pocket corner holds the cable, adapter and charger. The rest is drape and the knot's corners. Nothing else lives on it.

## 2. Keyboard range (job 3, 27)
- **Every keyboard, chunky minis included:** the only kit that carries them (owner trade-off 2).
- **No end blocks:** it holds by wrapping. **Nothing crosses a key:** the keys face the quilted sleeve, never a band.
- **Any socket position:** nothing hems the keyboard in, so no notch or gutter is needed.

## 3. Cable path
- **Spread:** the 0.3–0.5 m cable [r2] runs a few centimetres across the cloth from the keyboard's socket (rear or end) to the host port on the module's front face (centred). It never crosses hands, speaker or panel.
- **Friend:** the keyboard goes beside you (0.5 m) or onto a friend's lap on the optional coiled cable.
- **Packed:** cable and adapter in the pocket.

## 4. The split target and the line (job 6)
On the module (module §4); the stitched line beside the centre line repeats it (visual, turn 5).

## 5. Flows
**Unpack (a fixed ritual):**
1. Untie the knot.
2. Spread the cloth, outline facing you.
3. Lift off the keyboard (it's on top) onto the outline's front half.
4. Slip the module from its sleeve onto the back half, on the dashed line.
5. Cable (and adapter) from the pocket: keyboard → host port.
6. Switch on.
7. Play.

**Pack:** the reverse, in the same order.

**On a lap (honest):** the cloth drapes and stops being a frame. **The lap pose is two objects:** the keyboard on your lap, the module on the bench beside you, on the cable.

**Keyboard missing:** the module alone in its sleeve, knotted: the smallest bundle. **Swap:** wrap whatever keyboard you have; re-set the brass target. **Home:** nothing to undo.

## 6. States (carry)
| State | What happens |
|---|---|
| Spread on a bench or table | the outline frames the pair |
| On a lap | two objects; the cloth drapes |
| Wind | corner weights hold the cloth |
| Wet or dirty | sleeve and liner come out and wash (washable fabrics UNVERIFIED) |
| In a bag | soft: protection comes from the module's rim and the sleeve's quilt, not a shell. A hard knock on the keyboard's back is only padded by the cloth |

## 8. Owner objections and trade-offs, up front
1. **Size:** the module alone is about A4 long and goes in a laptop sleeve. With a keyboard, the bundle is keyboard-sized and soft.
2. **Weight:** the lightest kit. Still misses T5 with an Xkey [r2].
3. **Craft:** sewing.
4. **Home octave:** module §4.
5. **Lamp:** none (no lid). At night there are only the module's lit TAP ring and port light. Honest.
6. **Speaker:** sealed, published [r2].

## 9. Family reuse (job 28)
- **Unchanged:** the cloth, pocket and weights.
- **Per instrument:** the sleeve (its size) and the outline's back half (a deeper module grows it). The cloth knots round a module up to ≈ 450 long [calc].

# DROP: how it looks and feels (visual, idea level)

2026-11-22. PROPOSAL. Read with `how-it-works.md` (systems), which sets the geometry: 260 × 88 × 62, ≈ 37°, one pose. Picture: `sheet.png`. Source: `../_src/cathedral_sheets.py`.

## The idea, as you see it
One solid tool you pick up by its handle. From the end it's a drop of water lying on its side: a low lip at the front, the plate leaning back at the angle the parts give, and the back rounding into a pipe. The pipe holds everything heavy and everything acoustic. The silhouette is that one end face, so the concept succeeds or fails on it.

## Why the shape (not costume)
- **The round back is the speaker pipe.** Its diameter comes from the driver, and its length comes from the box, the two cells and the port. No curve is there for looks.
- **The angle isn't chosen.** It's the line from the knob stack to the top of the pipe.
- **The port mouth sits at the centre of the left end face**, and the growth-ring grooves are centred on it. The end reads as a log's end grain and an organ pipe's mouth at once. That's CATHEDRAL's end grain with a reason.
- **The director's watch: "a log behind a ramp".** Two things make the plate and the pipe read as one body.
  - **One rim line:** a bead runs round the whole end face, lip, slope and pipe together.
  - **One field:** on top, the cream plate doesn't stop at the pipe. It turns over the pipe's upper front like a page curling, inside the same 8 mm walnut end rims. The clerestory lancets (the two drivers) sit on that curl.
  - Seen from the player, it's one framed field that bends.

## Materials (all printed except the knobs)
- **Body:** printed walnut-brown (ASA or PETG from the spool), with growth-ring grooves printed into the end faces.
- **Plate field:** printed cream.
- **Grip strips:** printed TPU, black, under the front and rear of the base.
- **Knobs:** bought, black, one red.
- **Metal:** the printed port ring, only if cast counts.
- **No cloth, no spine.**

## Feel
- **Pick it up:** your hand closes round the pipe like a rolled newspaper. The weight sits low and at the back, under your fingers.
- **Set it down:** it lands on one face and stays there. There's nothing to unfold.
- **Play:** your wrist rests on the low lip and the plate rises away from it, like a music desk. At 37° the knobs face your eyes on a lap.
- **Packed:** the pipe takes the knocks and the rim guards the knobs. The keyboard rides keys-out under the flat base on the SHOE, or loose in the bag.

## Open
- **The biggest risk:** the round mass may read as a hair dryer or a doorstop rather than one rim. The sheet's end view is the test.
- It's the chunkiest of the three in the bag (62 tall).
- Metal: the port ring only if cast counts.

# DROP: how it works (systems, idea level)

2026-11-22. PROPOSAL. Rulings: director turn 5 (2 rows, ≈ 37°, 260 × 88 × 62). Sizes are [calc] from `eng/module-r3.md` facts. Nothing is measured.

## The idea
The back of floss is a pipe that holds everything heavy and acoustic. The plate rises from a low lip, tangent to that pipe. The angle isn't chosen: it's the line from the knob stack to the top of the pipe. You carry it by the pipe.

## Structure, from the end face
- **Lip ≈ 26.5:** r3's knob stack minus the cell layer, since the cells live in the pipe now. The host port is here.
- **Plate:** two rows of controls, ≈ 48 along the slope, tangent to the pipe's top. **θ ≈ 37°.**
- **Pipe:** Ø 56 inside, ≈ 62 outside, set by the driver (Ø 55 frame on a chord baffle, with 27 mm behind it).
- **Base:** flat, 88 deep. It carries the SHOE dock (S9) at y ≈ 10–70 and TPU grip strips at its front and rear edges.
- **Overall ≈ 260 × 88 × 62.** The pipe's length sets the width.

## What lives where (every volume has one job)
| Volume | Holds |
|---|---|
| Wedge under the plate | the board and the controls only |
| Pipe, left bay ≈ 65 | cell 1, with the port passing above it |
| Pipe, centre ≈ 120 | both drivers in one box, ≈ 250 cm³ net (eng's E2) |
| Pipe, right bay ≈ 65 | cell 2 |
| Port Ø 16 × 140, flared | from the box, through the left bay, out at the **centre of the left end face** |

## Speaker and ports
- **Speaker:** the two drivers fire forward and up through **lancet slots in the pipe's upper front**, just behind the plate's top edge. That puts the sound over the controls, toward the face, and keeps the plate for controls only.
- **USB-C host:** on the lip face, left of centre, ≈ 13 up. A straight plug clears the table.
- **Jacks, power switch, device USB-C, TUNE, MIDI SPLIT:** on the **right end face**, below the plate. The left end is the port mouth; the right end is the wires.

## Poses
- **One pose: on its base, at 37°.** The second pose (on the pipe) was cut by the director.
- **Table:** sits at about the angle of a music desk.
- **Lap:** flat base on the thighs; the steep face meets eyes looking down.
- **Grass:** the flat base sits evenly.
- **Stability [calc, mass ≈ 0.8 kg ESTIMATE]:**
  - a front-row press, or the hand on the lip, pushes into the footprint;
  - a hard top-row press tips it only above ≈ 26 N;
  - sliding needs the TPU strips (brass alone slides on a smooth table).
- **If the owner wants flat too:** DROP can't. It's the angled-only answer.

## Moves: bag to sound (3)
1. Out of the bag by the pipe.
2. Slide the keyboard off the SHOE dock (or take it from the bag).
3. Set DROP down, plug in, switch on.

**Packing:** reverse. 260 × 88 × 62; ≈ 70–75 with the keyboard docked. The pipe takes the knocks, and the rim guards the knobs, so there's no cover.

## States
- **No keyboard:** plays from USB or TRS; the dock is an empty recess.
- **Carried loose:** the kit is just DROP and a keyboard in the bag.
- **Low battery or charging:** as `jobs.md` (status light), unchanged by the form.

## Making
Printed body. NAVE bays are possible: seams on the section rules as engraved hairlines. The speaker box is its own airtight printed part. Metal appears only as the port-mouth ring, and only if "cast from printed wax" counts. Otherwise there's no metal.

## Owner forks
- **Flat too:** no → DROP is a candidate; yes → CASKET-B.
- **Carry:** SHOE / loose / TPU band (see `../c-shoe.md`).
- **Cast metal:** the port ring or none.

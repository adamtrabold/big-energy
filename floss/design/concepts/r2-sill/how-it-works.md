## The idea
CATHEDRAL's lid lying down, with one flush bar in its base. Flat, it's a calm slab on the table. Flip the bar down with a fingertip and the back lifts slightly. The bar is invisible from above, and nothing goes outside the outline except the bar itself, straight down under the back.

## Structure
- **Slab:** 280 × **115** × 35 (director: D 115, for speaker E's margin).
- **The bar:** printed, **≈ 236 × 17.5 × 3.5**, in a flush recess in the base's rear band (y ≈ 95–113).
  - **Why 236, not 280:** the n 5 plan corners (r 17.5) curve the rear edge in over ≈ 17.5 at each end, so a straight flush bar fits only the straight run, ≈ 280 − 2 × 17.5 − walls ≈ 236 [calc]. Stability doesn't need the full width: side-to-side tipping isn't critical.
  - **Knuckles:** **two, at x ≈ 30 and ≈ 250**, each Ø 9 × ≥ 20 (eng r3 §6d), on a bought Ø 3 steel pin or printed-metal stubs. They sit in pockets ≈ 10 deep, because a 9 mm knuckle can't fit the 3.5 mm recess. The plate load passes through the **full-length stop**, the recess's rear wall bearing on the bar's hinge edge, not through the knuckles. So **visual's middle knuckles (x 90 / 190) aren't needed**; fewer pockets means less of E's box lost.
  - **Finger notch:** centred at the bar's free edge (y ≈ 92–95), a shallow dimple in the base. Clear of the SHOE dock (y 10–70).
- **Travel:** 0° (flush) to a stop a few degrees past 90°. Tilted, the bar's foot sits behind its hinge, so plate loads drive it into the stop.
- **Detents:** a printed flexure tongue presses a flat on each knuckle, at 0° and at the stop. Fatigue is UNVERIFIED; fallback: two small magnets.
- **Feet:** TPU strips along the front foot and on the bar's edge.

## Stability
- **Flat:** whole base. Table, lap and grass are all fine.
- **Angled:** front foot plus a full-width bar line, ≈ 280 × 100. A knob press and the hand on the front edge land inside it. Lap: two full-width lines across both thighs. Grass ≈ 3.5 kPa under the bar's edge. Nothing tips at 6–10° [calc].

## CHOCK (inset only; a fallback, not a concept)
- **Visual's tapered strip (17.5 × 6 at the foot × 280) won't store:** 6 mm is thicker than the 3.5 recess, and 280 is longer than the 236 recess.
- **CHOCK that works [calc]:** a loose **236 × 17.5 × 3.5 uniform strip** (the bar without its knuckles). It stores flat in the same recess, held by the same detent bump. To tilt, its top edge **presses into a snug printed slot along the recess's rear wall**, so it stands upright on its own while you set the module down. Its 3.5 mm foot carries a TPU strip.
- **Trade:** no hinge, but it can be lost (then floss plays flat), and it's two hands to fit. It differs from SILL only by the hinge.
- **Recommendation:** keep it as the **fallback if neither a steel pin nor printed-metal stubs are acceptable**. One line on the owner sheet; drop the inset if space is short.

## Geometry drawn
280 × 104 × 52; plate at ≈ 9.46° (tan θ = T/2D, D 105, T 35); front face 35, square to the plate, leaning ≈ 5.8 forward; back vertical 51.8, base and back 90° (marked); hairline from the front-bottom edge up to ≈ 16.3 at the back. Angled only, no flat pose (director, turn 7).

## Looks
- **Body:** GNOMON rev 5's soft wedge, CATHEDRAL's rounding: n 5 plan corners r 17.5 on the upright faces, rolled edges on the plate and base, lip r 10, top-back r 6, base-back r 4 so the 90° reads.
- **The lid's rim:** the slab (the lid) stands **2 mm proud of the cheek** at each end, so the hairline is a shadow step, not a seam. Director's naming: the lid's rim, structure, not a book cue.
- **End face:** growth rings centred on the port mouth (≈ y 51, z 22, in the slab band), running across both the slab end and the cheek.
- **Plate:** two rows of 10 knobs (t 22 / 42), **one lancet grille** at the output (right) end of the rear band, power / TUNE / SPLIT mirroring it at the left end. Host USB-C on the front face.
- **Cheek (the wedge):** the speaker box and the cells: the shape's extra volume has a job.

## Feel
Low and planted: the steadiest of the round, nothing tips or slides it. Hands fall naturally on the front row; the grille sits far from the hands. Packed is play-ready.

## Honest read of the sketch (the director asked for it)
**At sketch size it reads as SILL tilted, without the leg.** In the 3/4 the 2 mm rim is a hairline at most; the one difference you see is the end face (a solid soft wedge with rings instead of a slab on a bar). The outline isn't more distinctive than SILL. I didn't add anything to make it look so: that would be decoration. Its case is calm and steady, not distinctive. That probably puts it under 9 by the director's own test.

## Trade-off (the sheet's caption)
The steadiest, zero parts, but no flat pose, and it reads close to SILL tilted.

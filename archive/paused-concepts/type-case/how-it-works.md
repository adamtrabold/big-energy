# TYPE CASE: how it works (systems)

Kind: **a container**. Pole: designed for use. Body: **self-protecting**. **Rev 5** (adopts visual round 2: sunk rules 1 mm below the floor, panel 24, rim 36.7, common layout rev 5). Rev 4: R4, no dividers proud, no front rail rising. Read with `../common-how-it-works.md` (rev 5) and `../../jobs.md`.

## The idea
A printer's type case is a shallow wooden tray divided into compartments; each holds one kind of sort, and the case is both the storage and the working surface. A synth panel is a case of sorts you compose sounds from. **Little D is a type case: a shallow tray whose divider walls are its circuit sections, with the controls living in the compartments.**

## Design principles (each follows from the idea)
1. **The walls are real, and sunk like rules.** The dividers are a structural egg-crate grid of slotted 3 mm ply strips under the panel; they rise into slots in the panel and **stop 1 mm below its face** (visual), leaving a 3 mm wide, 1 mm deep groove with the pale ply edge at its bottom. *Because* a type case's dividers hold things (these hold the panel up between pots), and in a forme the printer's rules sit below the type; the owner's hands never meet them (R4), but a fingertip can feel the groove (J16).
2. **One compartment per section, in reading order, numbered I–V.** *Because* a case is sorted by kind of sort; the circuit's kinds are its sections. Compartment size follows contents, so the lay (big II and IV, narrow I, III, V) comes from the circuit.
3. **The case protects its contents, from behind and beside.** The side and rear rims stand level with the knob tops; the front is open to the hands, like a type case pulled out of its cabinet and laid on the stone. *Because* a case keeps its sorts safe (no lid, no sleeve, J6), but you work a case from its open front (R4).
4. **Labels are case cards.** Each compartment carries a card (numeral + section name) lying **flat in a shallow pocket** in its front band under a flush 1 mm clear acrylic cover strip (a fingernail notch lifts it), letterpress-printed or hand-lettered, swappable. *Because* that's how cases are labelled, and it's where the owner's hand goes (J13, H6). Flat, not standing, so nothing rises in front of the knobs (R4).
5. **The front rail is the key edge (H3′), and it's flush.** The case's front rail is a flat 3 mm ply strip at panel height between the keys and the compartments, with the egg-crate's front ends slotted into its underside: it hides the switch housings and the back of strip C and holds the grid square. *Because* a case's front rail holds it square; it needn't stand up to do that, and the owner wants no separator (R4).

## Structure

**Plan:** common layout (298 × 180), common panel sentence (rev 5 layout: ≥ 7 mm from knob to break). Walls sit on the four breaks: x 41.5, 121, 172.9, 264.5.

| Compartment | x | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| Stick | 3–25 (stick column): the left rim on its outer side; a **flush** divider edge between it and the first key | bend stick + Delrin gate, MOD dial |
| Keys | 25–295, y 3–52 | 25 short tops on Choc V2 silent, surface ≈ 24 (R4), open at the front |
| I | 3–41.5 | TUNE, GLIDE, MOD MIX, 2 toggles |
| II | 41.5–121 | 3 VCO rows, OSC 3 CONTROL |
| III | 121–172.9 | mixer |
| IV | 172.9–264.5 | CUTOFF (192, 104, hero), EMPHASIS, CONTOUR AMT at 25 / 24 mm; filter and loudness A/D/S; KBD, VEL, DECAY toggles |
| V | 264.5–295 | VOLUME, status light |

- **Interior walls:** 3 mm ply on the breaks, **top 1 mm below the panel face** (sunk rule, R4 ✓, tactile ✓). They pass up through 3.2 mm slots; set the depth with a 1 mm spacer strip on the panel face during glue-up. Every knob edge is ≥ 7 mm from a rule (common rev 5). If 1 mm is too faint to feel, 1.5 mm (visual's fallback).
- **Worst knob (director fix 4): no longer an issue.** With sunk walls, every knob has its full 10.45 mm height free to grip; the nearest obstruction is the neighbouring knob (5 mm gap, R2).
- **Card pockets:** a 2 mm-deep pocket in each compartment's front band (y ≈ 80–93): card (≈ 0.5 mm) + a 1 mm clear acrylic cover strip, **flush with the panel** (R4). Card ≈ (compartment width − 4) × 12 mm. Cards read from above, flat.
- **Egg-crate:** 5 interior walls slot into the front rail's underside and the rear rail (half-depth slots); their top edges sit 1 mm down in the panel slots. Build: dry-fit grid → drop the panel over it with a 1 mm spacer → glue.

**Rim (fix 2): sides and rear only, z ≈ 36.7, level with CUTOFF's top** (visual), with a 1–2 mm chamfer or round on the outer top edge. No shaft trimming needed. **Body ≈ 36.7 = the rim; knob tops ≤ 36.65.** Inside R3 (≈ 36 body, ≤ 40 to knob tops).

**Speaker:** bottom, ≈ (170, 128), feet 6 mm. **Jacks:** rear wall, x 150–290. **Power switch:** recessed in the rear wall beside the jacks. **Status light:** compartment V. **"Not in control" light:** by CUTOFF in IV.

**Optional making rule (from ONE SHEET, director):** cut the whole tray from one 12 × 24 in sheet, nest map engraved on the bottom, recut any part from the file.

## Flows that differ from common
- **F1 / F5:** no open or close step.
- **F4 Keep a patch:** a panel-sized printed patch card stored in a shallow slot in the bottom (optional; or in the bag).
- **F10 Service:** bottom off (visible screws). The egg-crate sits above the board; the board lifts out after the knobs come off.
- **F11 Label:** panel legends engraved through ink-dyed floors back to pale wood (visual's method, H6); cards by hand or letterpress.

## Six axes
1 Layout: signal sentence, compartments in order. 2 Material: 3 mm birch ply, egg-crate, India-ink floors. 3 Posture: flat; the rim is the hand rest. 4 Protection: rim at knob height. 5 Speaker: bottom. 6 Labels: engraved floors + slide-in cards.

**Unsupported jobs: none.** Weak spots: J5 on a lap (set-wide), J15 drop front on a lap, J16 sections not tactile.

**Fit flag (not a job failure):** CUTOFF's top (36.65) sits above the lid's inner face (35); the lid pocket over CUTOFF must be **2.5 mm deep** (0.85 mm clearance), not 2 mm (0.35 mm). Visual/handoff to carry it.

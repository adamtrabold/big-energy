## The idea
The cigar-box guitar is the tradition of the cheap instrument built at home from a box; a hymnal is a book you carry to where you'll play, with what you'll play inside its cover. **Little D is the budget instrument in its own box: the lid opens up as a music desk carrying the patch chart, the front drops as a palm rest, and closed it's a labelled box with everything inside.**

## Design principles (each follows from the idea)
1. **The box is the protection.** Four full walls and the lid enclose everything, keys included. *Because* a box needs no bag (J6).
2. **It opens where the hands go.** The lid stands up behind the panel at ≈ 104° as a desk; the front wall is hinged at its bottom edge and drops flat as a palm rest. *Because* a box that becomes an instrument must open fully at the front and stand up at the back.
3. **What you'll play is kept inside the cover.** The inside of the lid is a patch chart (the panel drawn 1:1 in sentence order, for pencil), facing the player when the lid stands. *Because* a hymnal carries its songs inside; no recall, so this box carries its sounds (J4).
4. **The label is the identity, and it's his.** Outside the lid: the owner's own label in a plain ruled border (placeholder "your title here" until he letters it). *Because* cigar boxes are known by their labels; a borrowed script would be costume (director fix 4).
5. **Old in the box, new on the panel.** The box is warm wood and paper edging; the panel is crisp birch with engraved legends, **neutral/black KN1250 knobs with a cream CUTOFF hero; red only for numerals**. *Because* the box carries the warmth; the instrument inside is modern (director fix 3).

## Structure

**Plan:** common layout (298 × 180), common panel sentence at R2 spacing. **Short tops** default (fix 1); piano-length tops are a small labelled "+$30" inset (stabilisers + plate) [ENG S2].

**Box:** the owner's walnut offcuts or 3 mm ply, oiled (visual round 2), 298 × 180 × **35** to the wall tops.

| Part | Hinge | Size |
|---|---|---|
| **Lid** | 2 small brass hinges on the rear edge; ribbon stay to ≈ 104° | 298 × 180 × 3; a 2 mm pocket in its underside over CUTOFF |
| **Drop front** | **cloth-tape flap hinge** (fix 2) along its bottom edge, on the box's front bottom edge | 298 × 35 × 3 |
| Sides, rear | fixed | 37 tall |

**How the drop front stays shut (fix 5): the lid holds it.** The lid's underside carries a 3 × 3 mm batten along its front edge, set 3 mm in from the edge. Closed, the batten sits *behind* the drop front's top edge, so the front can't fall open while the lid is shut. Order: **open the lid first, then drop the front; raise the front, then close the lid.** No catch hardware on the front.

**How the lid stays shut:** two 6 mm magnets in the lid's front corners over steel washers in the side-wall tops (x ≈ 5 and 293, y ≈ 8). They are ≥ 25 mm from the nearest key sensor and only near it when the lid is closed (not playing); a static field the per-key rest learning absorbs at wake [ENG keys-answers §6 logic]. **The elastic band is dropped** (fix: rigmarole). Parts count: 2 hinges, 1 tape hinge, 1 ribbon, 2 magnet catches.

## States
| State | Form |
|---|---|
| Carry | closed: lid down (held by the magnets), front held up by the lid's batten. A plain labelled box |
| Play | lid up at ≈ 104° (ribbon taut), patch chart facing the player; front dropped flat as a palm rest |
| Lid up, front up | allowed (e.g. reading the chart); the front stands by its own hinge friction or leans; harmless |
| Shelf (J18) | stands on its left end (jacks are on the right), label out, like a book |

## Flows that differ from common
- **F1:** lid up (magnets release) → ribbon stops it → drop the front → power (right wall) → play.
- **F5 Pack:** power off → raise the front → close the lid (the batten captures the front; magnets hold the lid).
- **F4 Keep a patch:** pencil on the lid's chart; the chart is a printed sheet under four corner pockets, replaceable.
- **F8 Patch:** cables leave the right wall behind the key edge; they don't cross the keys or the stick.
- **F10 Service:** bottom off (visible screws); hinges stay.
- **F12 Shelf:** on its left end.

## Six axes
1 Layout: signal sentence. 2 Material: 3 mm ply box, oil, brass hinges, cloth-tape hinge, paper edging. 3 Posture: closed / open (desk + palm rest). 4 Protection: the box. 5 Speaker: bottom. 6 Labels: panel engraved; label and patch chart printed (letterpress) or hand-lettered.

# Controls menu: how it looks and feels (visual), round 2

Sheet: `controls-menu.png`. Systems' mechanics: `how-it-works.md` (its printed
options C1–C3, C6, C8 are retired by the owner's "real parts").

**Checked against:** owner.md ("real parts"; "can we get creative with any of
the knob / wheel styles? wheels seem to be cheapest"; the bend/mod brainstorm:
sticks, "tiny joysticks", touch strip, fader, "little dials"; the pick:
"joystick is great if we can go one direction", bend springs back, mod stays

- **Knobs (bought):** skirted knurled (default), soft-touch, collet + coloured
  cap, knurled aluminium, chicken-head, large skirted hero, and the draw result
  (bookbinding × "faced with a choice, do both"): one bought knob type with a
  glued leather band on every rotating edge.
- **Bend/mod LEAD (on every concept mock):** a spring-return thumbstick under a
  fore-aft slot (the gate makes it one-directional; only Y is read) for bend,
  plus a 20 mm mod fader behind it (or a small dial). 13 mm zone at the left of
  the keys; DOCK's pod beside the panel. Stick body fit under 13 mm:
  UNVERIFIED (mini stick fallback).
- **Alternatives laid out side by side:** thumbwheels, classic wheels, X/Y
  stick, two sticks, mini sticks (must be analog), touch strips, mod fader,
  little dials, plus mixes.
- **Faders and selectors:** slide pot + fader cap (default), abacus with bought
  beads (MOTTO option), Lorlin rotary switch vs pot with firmware detents.

## The idea, as it should read

One flat, stiff slab, like a hardback book with a cover you lift off. Nothing
on it moves except the knobs and keys. The cover becomes the stand: a familiar
trick (a tablet folio, a typewriter's lid) that costs nothing and has no wrong
way. The draw for this round's stand ("typewriters") confirmed the reading: a
portable typewriter's lid comes off and the machine sits ready.

## Looks

- **Body:** Field look in the mock: laser-cut ply layers faced in warm-grey
  felt, cork base layer. Any look on the look sheet fits SLAB.
- **Panel:** 280 × 100 bare PCB sunk 4 mm, black mask, cream silk, gold
  rules; orange hero.
- **Strip between panel and keys (14 mm):** body top, carrying the wordmark at
  the right and the amber pip note in the mock; two grey lid-magnet dots at
  the ends.
- **Keys:** K-B levers, cream tops, warm-grey sharps with a glow stripe at the
  front. Wheels: two thumbwheels in the 13 mm well at the left.
- **Lid:** two felt leaves joined by a sewn leather strip; plain on top with
  the wordmark. Carried, it reads as one closed block 285 × 185 × 46.

## Feel and motion, by state

| State | What you see / feel |
|---|---|
| carried (lid on) | flat block, no protrusions; the lid's end magnets hold it with a soft click (felt-faced, no metal-on-metal) |
| lid lifted | status LED breathes within 200 ms; the lid comes away whole |
| folding the stand | the leather strip bends; the leaves meet on magnets (soft thud) |
| stand on | press it under the rear: four magnets on steel discs; the body tilts 8°, rear row ≈ 25 mm higher |
| playing flat (lap) | no stand; the body is rigid, nothing to hinge or sag |
| MIDI owns a ringed control | the single amber pip by HOLD lights (V-a); move ringed knobs until it goes out, or HOLD + TUNE |
| lights off | glow pointers, sharp stripes, pip, status LED (inset) |

## Why (reasoning for the handoff's Why)

- R2: SLAB must differ from DOCK in kind; the mock shows the lid as a stand and
  the fold sequence, so the difference is visible.
- One pip is the cheapest honest light for a designed-for-use instrument: it
  answers "is something not in control?" without decorating the panel.
- Leather hinge: Vector Space has the sewing machines; it is silent and
  replaceable; and the controls-menu draw (bookbinding) gives the leather a
  second job on knob bands if the owner wants it.

## Open

- 8° vs 12° tilt (12° costs ≈ +15 mm carry).
- Stand stiffness under a 20 N press at the rear row.
- Service-cut key tops: owner's OK, else K-A flat keys.

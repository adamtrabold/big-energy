# synths-to-go principles

These apply to every instrument and aren't reopened per instrument. Only Adam changes them. His verbatim record is `design/owner.md`. Targets, parts and features belong in each instrument's requirements. An agent's proposal is not a principle.

## Goal
Portable synthesizers Adam designs with agents and builds himself.

**Analog-first.** "The goal is analog-first, but that doesn’t mean digital won’t be used or should be completely avoided." (2026-11-22)

## 1. Portable
- "The principles are portable." There's no fixed weight number; lighter is better.
- Fits comfortably in a backpack.
- Played away from a desk ("take this to the park").
- A keyboard of 25 keys or fewer.

## 2. Harmonious proportion
- Proportions come from a deliberate ratio derived from the device itself, and it needn't be a rectangle. "It just needs to be portable and make sense and be harmonious."
- Grids and scales derive from the device's own dimensions.
- Examples aren't targets: "don’t anchor on the page size thing."

## 3. Sound module plus a carried keyboard
- "portable sound modules with a way to carry a keyboard with it. It should be flexible and not reliant on a single model of keyboard." This applies to every instrument, Big Energy included.
- No built-in keys or buttons on the module.
- The module is sized by its own needs, not the keyboard's width.
- The keyboard connects over USB-C, with the module as host. The carry and connection are one family standard.

## 4. Shared universe
- "make these as a shared universe and reuse as much as we can."

## 5. Design from the idea of the thing
- Form follows "a solid basis of logic in the idea of the thing", with a clear point of view and no decoration.
- Inspiration: furniture, instruments, tools, art.
- Explore broadly, using real randomness. Nod to classics; don't copy them.

## 6. Panels make sense
- Signal order: synth, then effects, then output.
- Related controls sit together.
- No arbitrary off-centre elements in the middle of a face.
- Section dividers mustn't get in the way of the hands.

## 7. Building
- Adam builds it, and it must be feasible for him. No machining.
- **For now, the case is entirely 3D printed** (2026-11-22). Printing by an outside service is fine. "Cloth wrapping is fine as long as the core is printed."
- Agents only: Adam measures nothing. Choices rest on published evidence or on virtual models built from it. Anything that needs a measurement is unverified.
- Reliable choices: proven parts and approaches.
- Surface mount is fine if necessary.
- Work from first principles; don't assume.
- Order of work: requirements, then an engineering check, then design.

## 8. Cost
- No fixed ceiling. Cost everything honestly and compare with the market.

## 9. Decisions
- Trade-offs go to Adam.
- Keep Adam's words separate from agent proposals.

## 10. Showing the work
- Images only where they're needed; text stays text.
- Made for the site, viewed responsively.
- No Claude attribution on GitHub.

## Family defaults (reused unless Adam changes them)
- MIDI: USB plus TRS in and out; settings controllable over MIDI; MIDI never flips switches; knob-only controls send no MIDI; the echo syncs to MIDI clock (FREE / SYNC).
- No screens or encoders (on trial).
- Battery and a built-in speaker, muted by headphones.
